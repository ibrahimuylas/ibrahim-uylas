# Spec 026 — Expand Western Likya GPS candidate research

**Status:** In progress  
**Priority:** P0 — evidence collection  
**Depends on:** Specs 022–025  
**Scope:** Internal candidate intake only; no public stages, GPS actions, or itinerary recommendations

## Goal

Extend the Western research queue with independently published Wikiloc records that have clear title/waypoint overlap with official Paftas 1, 2, 4, and 5.

## Candidate safeguards

- Every record remains `candidate` and `research`, even where its title closely resembles a pafta title.
- Pafta 1’s trace continues beyond Kirme to Faralya; it cannot be treated as an exact stage boundary.
- Pafta 4’s trace starts at “Bel”, while the official title starts at “Belceğiz”; this naming/boundary relation requires review.
- Pafta 5’s author describes a composite research process, so first-hand traversal/provenance must be confirmed before verification.
- No track statistics, water waypoints, terrain claims, user comments, or time estimates are imported into guide content.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Each candidate is attributed to its original author and Wikiloc record.
- [ ] Recorded and uploaded dates are stored separately.
- [ ] The research board marks only Paftas 1–5 where candidates exist as `research`; none becomes eligible.
- [ ] Release validation stays on hold and no public GPS action is enabled.
