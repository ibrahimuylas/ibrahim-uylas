# Spec 025 — Seed Western Likya GPS candidate research

**Status:** In progress  
**Priority:** P0 — evidence collection  
**Depends on:** Specs 022–024  
**Scope:** Internal candidate intake only; no published stage or itinerary

## Goal

Start the Western Likya research batch with source-backed external-GPS candidates, while keeping every unverified route claim out of the visitor experience.

## First intake

Create a candidate record for Wikiloc’s Alınca–Sidyma–Bel trace and attach it only to official Pafta 3. The record remains `research` because the official title ends at Gey-Bel while the third-party record identifies Bel; coverage, route direction, variation choice, and current usability must be reviewed before it becomes `verified`.

## Rules

- Preserve the trace’s original author, recorded/uploaded dates, and URL.
- Do not transfer its distance, elevation, water waypoints, difficulty, or commentary into public route data during candidate intake.
- Do not infer that the dated trace reflects current markings, water availability, closures, or the official path.
- Treat candidate status as a research lead. It must leave the public guide, planner, stage cards, and GPS download actions unchanged.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The candidate is linked to the matching official pafta and its original Wikiloc source.
- [ ] The title/endpoint difference is recorded as an unresolved review item.
- [ ] The research board shows Pafta 3 as `research`, without marking any checklist item complete.
- [ ] Data validation passes and release status remains withheld.
