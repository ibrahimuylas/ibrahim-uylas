# Publish the Eastern Likya guide content package

**Type:** Content package

**Priority:** P1 — closes the full-route gap beyond the site's 2014 walked section

**Risk:** High

## Goal

Research, author and connect every official route section in the Eastern editorial region, including sections for which the site currently has little or no first-hand content.

The provisional scope begins around the Demre/Finike handoff and continues through the official eastern terminus established in Spec 009. The 2014 plan mentions Finike, Kumluca, Karaöz, Gelidonya, Adrasan, Olympos/Çıralı, Tekirova, Beycik, Gedelme, Göynük and Hisarçandır; these notes are leads to verify, not a current route definition.

## Research Deliverables

For every official section in scope:

- official map order, route branches and terminus;
- current official/source-qualified distance and elevation;
- GPS coverage with owner, date, direction and branch;
- terrain, sustained climb/descents and exposure;
- water/service records with observation dates;
- accommodation modes and legal/operational uncertainty;
- transport access and exit options;
- forest/fire restriction source path;
- historical-site or protected-area access context;
- same-week verification checklist.

Because this region extends beyond İbrahim's actual 2014 walk, do not write in first person unless the repository contains a genuine dated experience for that exact section.

## Authoring Deliverables

1. Eastern region overview with fit, difficulty profile, logistics, branches, seasonal cautions and full stage index.
2. One complete shared-template stage page for every practical stage needed to cover the official sections.
3. Explicit “editorial research guide” attribution where first-hand experience is absent.
4. Clear links to any relevant historical 2014 planning notes with a label that they were planned, not necessarily walked.
5. Source/freshness records for every changing claim.
6. Candidate short/full-route plan combinations for Spec 017.

## Content Quality Rules

- Require at least two independent sources for material route interpretations when one is not the official route source; one should be primary/official where available.
- Do not fill knowledge gaps with generic hiking advice.
- Do not convert a third-party itinerary into the site's recommendation without original editorial evaluation.
- Mark unresolved variants and data gaps publicly or hold the affected stage from “complete” status.
- Explain difficult mountain or remote sections using effort and escape implications, not sensational language.

## Responsive Requirements

- Mountain/branch complexity must be summarized in readable mobile text before map interaction.
- Long stage sets use region subsections and a compact progress/navigation pattern; no enormous desktop table shrunk onto mobile.
- Source and “not personally walked” labels remain visible on cards and stage headers.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Every official Eastern section through the verified eastern terminus has a source-backed record and stage-guide path.
2. Sections not personally walked by İbrahim are never narrated as first-hand experience.
3. The difference between the 2014 planned eastern itinerary and verified modern route coverage is explicit.
4. GPS, route variants, water/services, transport/exit and forest/fire verification paths are documented for every stage or marked unresolved.
5. No unresolved stage is counted as complete in the guide launch checklist.
6. All pages pass the shared template, responsive, accessibility and source validation requirements.

## Out of Scope

- Inventing personal observations.
- Live fire, closure or transport feeds.
- Final plan recommendation ranking.
