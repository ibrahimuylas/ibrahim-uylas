# Publish the Central Likya guide content package

**Type:** Content package

**Priority:** P1 — completes the middle route and the site's strongest first-hand archive coverage

**Risk:** High

## Goal

Research, author and connect every official route section in the Central editorial region using the shared source model and stage template.

The provisional scope begins around the Patara/Kınık/Kalkan handoff, continues through Kaş and Kekova/Kaleüçağız, and hands off around Demre/Finike according to the verified official-map inventory. Spec 009 defines the final boundary.

- relevant Kalkan, Kaş, Kekova, Üçağız, Demre and ancient-site material.

Correct known labelling/date inconsistencies only after checking the source and journal chronology.

## Research Deliverables

For every official section in scope, record the same mandatory fields as Spec 014, with additional attention to:

- coastal versus inland branches;
- high-effort ascent/descent transitions;
- points where road transfer, boat assumption or ambiguous historical naming could mislead the user;
- protected/archaeological-area access context;
- sparse-water and limited-service claims;
- public-transport and car-recovery implications between small settlements.

Do not treat a GPS platform's popular track as the official branch without corroboration.

## Authoring Deliverables

1. Central region overview with fit, terrain, logistics, season limits, branches and complete stage navigation.
2. One complete shared-template page for every practical stage needed for official coverage.
3. A dated “İbrahim'in 2014 yürüyüşü” link on the seven existing first-hand stages.
4. Clear separation between planned routes and the route actually walked.
5. Source/freshness records for water, services, transport and access.
6. Candidate short-plan combinations for Spec 017, especially routes that can begin/end with realistic transport.

## Content Style

- Prioritize route choice, daily load, water/logistics uncertainty and exit strategy.
- Ancient sites and views support the decision; they do not bury the practical answer.
- Where historic place/route names vary, show the preferred label and aliases once.
- Use a short field-note excerpt only when it adds a concrete lesson.
- Avoid recommending solo travel, camping locations or swimming/boat transfers as universally suitable.

## Responsive Requirements

- Route branches must be understandable in text before a map is loaded.
- Mobile day-flow and transport content uses vertical steps and cards.
- Long source/status text collapses only after its date and state remain visible.
- Existing field images use accurate captions and responsive image handling.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Every official Central section from the verified boundary has a source-backed record and a complete navigation path.
2. The seven existing practical stage URLs are preserved or explicitly mapped without losing their matching journals.
3. The 230 km walked route is accurately connected to the relevant modern stages without being presented as full-route coverage.
4. Route branches, GPS coverage and transport/exit implications are explicit and source-qualified.
5. No changing condition is published without review metadata and no historical claim is visually mistaken for a current one.
6. All pages pass shared template, responsive, accessibility and source validation.

## Out of Scope

- Western/Eastern prose.
- Final recommendation ranking.
- Live boat, bus or accommodation schedules.
