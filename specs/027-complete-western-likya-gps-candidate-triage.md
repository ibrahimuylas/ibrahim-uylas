# Spec 027 — Complete Western Likya GPS candidate triage

**Status:** In progress  
**Priority:** P0 — evidence collection  
**Depends on:** Specs 022–026  
**Scope:** Internal candidate intake only; no route publication

## Goal

Add candidate evidence for official Paftas 6–9, and explicitly leave Pafta 10 unmapped because the available Hacıoğlan–Çukurbağ–Kaş trace does not establish the official Dereköy–Çukurbağ boundary.

## Triage decisions

- Pafta 6: a longer Xanthos–Akbel record passes through Üzümlü and İslamlar. It is a broad-coverage lead, not an exact stage.
- Pafta 7: Akbel–Delikkemer–Patara directly overlaps the official endpoints, but remains a candidate until geometry and current alignment are reviewed.
- Pafta 8: Kalkan–Bezirgan–Sarıbelen directly overlaps the official title, but its dated logistics must not be imported.
- Pafta 9: Sarıbelen–Gökçeören–Hacıoğlan supplies a lead for the official Hacıoğlan pafta, whose scope is incomplete in the official inventory.
- Pafta 10: no suitable external candidate is accepted in this batch.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Paftas 6–9 become `research`; Pafta 10 remains `unmapped`.
- [ ] All records preserve original author, record date, upload date, and external-only delivery.
- [ ] Broad/compound traces carry an explicit scope or provenance review note.
- [ ] No candidate is eligible, published, or visible to guide visitors.
