Important, please read:
Although we will always keep in mind the colour, markings and gender preferences of our puppy buyers, we do not place our pups based solely on colour, markings or gender.  We evaluate the pups temperament, structure and toy drive and puppies are matched with their new owners.  The selection is determined by 6-7 weeks of age.  Reservations are honoured and based upon suitability of each pup to each individual home.
​
Any payments made to reserve a puppy are non-refundable.
​
Also please remember we do NOT guarantee a pups height once fully grown. We are dealing with mother nature and although we can give a good estimate at 8 weeks this can never be guaranteed and should not be the deciding factor when getting a MAS.
We do not have any kennels and each litter is born in our bedroom before they are moved to the living room at around 3-4 weeks of age into a large puppy play pen. They will be exposed to a variety of different environments, toys, & surfaces (including a wobble board and wobble cushion). They will be given "
early neurological stimulation
".  They regularly go in our garden in a large "puppy kindergarden" with even more wobble cushions and a large wobble bridge. All of our puppies will be litter box and crate trained and they will travel in a vehicle.  Our litters are registered with AKC and will be legible for registration with The Kennel Clubs Activity Register as a Miniature American Shepherd .
