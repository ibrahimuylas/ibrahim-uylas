The ladies at Basileas Minis
All of our girls are fully health tested prior to breeding which includes a full DNA breed panel, hips & elbows xrayed, patellas checked and cleared of LTV.
We are lucky to work with great placement families so not all of our girls live with us but are placed in their own families where they enjoy individual attention and training without ever being held in kennels. This helps us maintain a big and healthy gene pool.
