The Gentlemen at Basileas Minis
Our boys do not live with us but are placed in loving family homes where they get to live as part of the family without the stress of girls in season and being kept separate during that time. They are all available to our breeding and for a very limited amount of outside females by prior arrangement only.
Our boys are registered with ASDR and/or AKC as Miniature American Shepherds.
