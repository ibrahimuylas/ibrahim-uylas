## Kaş–Körmen Island: A short stage along the shore of Limanağzı

This guide covers the 13 km tenth stage from Kaş via Limanağzı and Ufakdere to the shore opposite Körmen Island. It was shorter than the previous day, but the coastal trail, limited recorded water and food options and accumulated fatigue shaped my plans.

> Historical field record: The following practical information is based on the experience of 29 September 2014. Water, facility, access, and camping conditions should not be considered current information. The price from 2014 is not the current price.

### Stage summary

- **Stage:** 10 of 11
- **Start and finish:** Kaş to Körmen Island
- **Waypoints:** Limanağzı and Ufakdere
- **Distance:** 13 km based on signposted distances
- **Walking time:** The total walking time is unknown. In my journal I noted that I set off around midday and pitched my tent at about 17:00.
- **Walked:** 29 September 2014
- **Current conditions:** No recent verification was available for this guide.

### Terrain, difficulty and navigation

The text describes a challenging, sea-parallel path in the Limanağzı approach in 2014. There was a clear loss of direction, but not over a long distance; instead, the fatigue of the previous nine days was felt.

### Water and food — 2014 record

I left Kaş with 3 litres of water and wrote that Ufakdere was the only place I found water along the way, and that it cost money. I also noted that food could be in short supply. I recorded water on the flat ground opposite Körmen Island. Current conditions and the price stated in 2014 have not been verified.

### Accommodation, camping and ways off the route — 2014 record

The camp was set up on a flat area across from Gece Körmen Island, in a tent. There was no recorded option for regular accommodation or departure during the phase.

### Notable places

The views of Kaş coastline, Limanağzı, Ufakdere, and Körmen Island are prominent features of this section.

### What I learned on this stage

The journal entries indicate a change in the initial goal. The author has abandoned the aim of reaching Üçağız and instead decided to settle on Körmen Island, based on the daylight and fatigue.

- After passing Limanağzı, there’s a place called Küçükdere. You can find water here, but 1.5 liters of water costs 4 TL.
- For the last 3 km before Limanağzı, the trail follows the sea and gets a little tricky, though it’s manageable.
- There is a flat area across from Körmen island, where there is water.
- I spent the night in a tent on the flat area across from Körmen Island.
- In this section of the route, you may experience a lack of food. Be sure to have something with you before you set out.
