Msr Pocket Rocket Camp Stove

This stove, produced by MSR, uses screw-type butane/propane cylinders and is remarkably lightweight and compact. According to measurements, it can boil 1 liter of water in 3.5 minutes. Using a 250gr cylinder, it can operate for approximately 60 minutes and boil around 16 liters of water. These measurements were naturally tested using a thin aluminum kettle. If you were to try boiling water in a steel kettle at home, you would, of course, obtain different results.

Why Choose It?

If you enjoy trekking and camping, or if you like to travel with your motorcycle and are looking for a small camping stove, this is an ideal choice for you. Weighing just 80gr, this stove fits comfortably in your hand. The wind panels ensure that the fire continues to burn even in windy conditions, keeping the flame steady.

Although it’s expensive compared to similar products, it’s a product that’s worth the money thanks to its quality and features.

**Advantages**

* Lightweight
* It’s small.
* Resistance to the wind
* High quality and durable
