### Where is Kayaüstü Highland and how do you get there?

In Kocaeli take the Yuvacık turn-off towards the reservoir. Follow the road uphill on the right-hand side. At the fork at the top, look for the sign for Tepecik village, Kayaüstü and İnönü Highland. Follow it through Tepecik, then turn right uphill at the mosque. Continue towards İnönü Highland until you see the sign for Kayaüstü Highland, and follow that to the pasture.

### Camping at Kayaüstü Highland

##### Available facilities

- Camping (There’s no campsite operator. You can pitch a tent anywhere on the highland pasture.)
- Clean water

##### Facilities that are unavailable

- The grocery store/market needs to be handled in Yuvacık.
- Restaurant
- Café
- Guesthouse
- Toilets

Note: This information dates back to 2014.

### Hiking routes around Kayaüstü Highland

#### From İnönü Highland to Kayaüstü Highland, and then on to Tepecik village (10.5 km).

- You should set off on your way, taking water from the İnönü Highland.
- Using forest tracks and tractor paths, you will first reach Kayaüstü Highland. Then, you will arrive at Tepecik village via a steep descent.
- A moderately challenging, enjoyable forest trail.

#### From Tepecik village, then to Kayaüstü Highland, and finally to Aytepe (19 km).

<iframe

- To avoid water shortages, you should collect your water before setting out.
- The route starts at Tepecik village and climbs steeply over 1.5 km to Kayaüstü Highland. It then continues through the forest to Aytepe.
- The first 1.5 km will involve an elevation gain of 350 meters, which may be a bit tiring.
- The last 5 km are downhill, so the going gets easier.
- A moderately difficult, long course. For beginners, its length makes the course quite challenging.

#### From Tepecik village to Kayaüstü Highland, and then back to Tepecik village (6.5 km).

- To avoid water shortages, you should collect your water before setting out.
- This route begins in Tepecik village and gradually ascends within the forest, eventually reaching Kayaüstü Highland. Afterwards, it descends steeply for 1.5 km, returning you to Tepecik village.
- The last 1.5km involves a 350-meter descent, which will be quite tiring.
- This is a challenging course in winter, and a much easier one in summer.
- If you’re going in winter, you need to use snowshoes.

#### From Tepecik village, then to the Kayaüstü Highland, and finally to the Yuvacık dam (24.5 km).

- To avoid water shortages, you should collect your water before setting out.
- This route, starting in Tepecik village, follows the İnönü Highland road and first reaches Kayaüstü Highland. Then, this route, which will gradually descend through the forest, reaches the starting point of the Yuvacık dam, passing through Kazandere.
- It’s an easy course, but its length of 24km might be a bit tiring for beginners.
