### Where is Kayaüstü valley, and how do you get there?

After arriving in Kocaeli, you continue from the Yuvacık fork towards the Yuvacık dam. When you reach the top of the dam on the right, you will see signs for Tepecik Village, Kaya Üstü, and İnönü Valley at the fork in the road. Following this road, after passing through Tepecik Village, you will reach İnönü Valley. Continuing along the road, following the Kaya Üstü Valley sign, you can reach the valley.

### Kayaüstü valley campsite information

##### Available facilities

- Camp Site (There is no specific business/establishment. You can set up a tent anywhere in the meadow.)
- Clean water

##### Facilities that are unavailable

- The grocery store/market needs to be handled in Yuvacık.
- Restaurant
- Café
- Guesthouse
- Toilets

Note: This information dates back to 2014.

### Kayaüstü Valley Hiking Routes

#### From İnönü plateau to Kayaüstü plateau, and then on to Tepecik village (10.5 km).

- You should set off on your way, taking water from the İnönü plateau.
- Using forest tracks and tractor paths, you will first reach Kaya Üstü Valley. Then, you will arrive at Tepecik village via a steep descent.
- A moderately challenging, enjoyable forest trail.

#### From Tepecik village, then to Kayaüstü valley, and finally to Aytepe (19 km).

  style={{ borderRadius: '10px 10px' }}

- To avoid water shortages, you should collect your water before setting out.
- The route starts at Tepecik village and climbs steeply over 1.5 km to Kaya Üstü Highland. It then continues through the forest to Aytepe.
- The first 1.5 km will involve an elevation gain of 350 meters, which may be a bit tiring.
- It would be easier if it continues as the last 5km.
- A moderately difficult, long course. For beginners, its length makes the course quite challenging.

#### From Tepecik village to Kayaüstü valley, and then back to Tepecik village (6.5 km).

- To avoid water shortages, you should collect your water before setting out.
- This route begins in Tepecik village and gradually ascends within the forest, eventually reaching Kaya Üstü valley. Afterwards, it descends steeply for 1.5 km, returning you to Tepecik village.
- The last 1.5km involves a 350-meter descent, which will be quite tiring.
- This is a challenging course in winter, and a much easier one in summer.
- If you’re going in winter, you need to use snowshoes.

#### From Tepecik village, then to the Kayaüstü valley, and finally to the Yuvacık dam (24.5 km).

- To avoid water shortages, you should collect your water before setting out.
- This route, starting in Tepecik village, follows the İnönü valley road and first reaches Kaya Üstü valley. Then, this route, which will gradually descend through the forest, reaches the starting point of the Yuvacık dam, passing through Kazandere.
- It’s an easy course, but its length of 24km might be a bit tiring for beginners.
