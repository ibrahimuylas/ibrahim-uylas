### Where is Dupnisa Cave, and how do you get there?

Take the TEM motorway from İstanbul towards Kırklareli, then turn onto the Saray road. Go through Poyralı to Demirköy. In Demirköy, turn left at the signs for Dupnisa Cave and continue via Sarpdere. Google may suggest shorter routes, but I find this one easiest.

### Dupnisa Cave camping area information

##### Available facilities

- Camp Site (You can set up your tent in the flat areas you find within the forest. There is no special business/facility here.)
- Clean water
- Toilets
- The entrance fee to the cave is 3 TL per person (2013).
- Don't underestimate this cave; it’s a massive cave, as large as those you see in movies, with a river flowing through it. You won't be able to help but be amazed. It’s a place that must be seen.

##### Facilities that are unavailable

- Groceries (stock up in Demirköy).
- Café (stop in Demirköy).
- You may encounter wild boars.
- Guesthouse
- Restaurant

Note: This information dates back to June 2013.

- The route begins in Çukurca village and proceeds through the forest, eventually reaching the Dupnisa cave.
- There are climbs and descents. It’s a moderately difficult trail.
- Pack water before you set off.
