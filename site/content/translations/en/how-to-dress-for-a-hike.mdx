The answer to this question depends on the weather. You need to dress according to the weather. It’s also definitely difficult to say exactly what to wear. Because there are now a large number of trekking outfits available, with varying qualities in the market. And you should choose the one that is suitable for you.

### So what should you wear in what weather?

During the summer months, the weather is beautiful, isn’t it? It’s warm and sunny during the day, so you’ll sweat more than usual. Therefore, you should opt for thermal clothing instead of cotton. This will allow the shirt on your body to wick away your sweat and not stick to your skin, resulting in a more comfortable walk. There’s not much to say about summer. It’s beneficial to choose thermal clothing. Or, simply, you should opt for comfortable clothing. If all else fails, take spare clothes with you. How much space will a shirt and, at most, a pair of shorts take up?

During the spring months; the weather conditions that worry me the most occur in the spring. In the mornings, it’s sunny, but in the afternoon, rain can start. You can get cold in the evenings when it’s warm. And then, of course, a cold. So, what should we wear? Do you know about the layered cabbage? Yes, exactly like that – you should dress like cabbage, in layers. First, a thermal shirt, then a warm fleece or jumper, and if there’s a chance of rain or wind, an additional raincoat. That way, if you get cold, you can put on your fleece, and if there’s a lot of wind or a light rain, you’ll put on your raincoat and keep yourself safe.

For the lower part, you can opt for trekking pants. Or, you can start your hike in the morning in shorts and finish it in the evening with a long outfit. Sweatpants would also work. My recommendation is trekking pants, but it’s perfectly fine otherwise. We will always try to suggest something better.

I dress as I described before. If the weather gets colder, I wear a t-shirt and shorts over my thermal underwear, and I also have a fleece and tracksuit with me to keep warm. When the weather cools down, I wear those. Of course, if the temperature is around 5 degrees. Otherwise, you’ll sweat unnecessarily.

During the winter months; winter months are very important, my friend. Don’t go climbing without solid equipment. Especially when there’s a lot of snow, you’ll be rescued in a natural setting, but if you’re camping, you need to be much more careful. No one wants to lose an organ due to the cold, right? My advice is to still wear thermal underwear when hiking. If it’s very cold, you can wear wool underwear. However, remember that if you sweat, wool underwear will absorb all the sweat and invite illness. You should dress in layers, like a lettuce pattern. This way, you’ll be better protected from the cold, and if you sweat excessively, you can simply remove the first layer. You should choose appropriate equipment for winter. It’s very important that your shoes don’t leak. You don’t want to go anywhere with wet feet, but when you’re on the mountain, you have to go, and then you’ll want to go trekking again.

To sum up, no matter what you wear, don’t forget the “cabbage” layering style. And also, there’s an accessory that can save your life in spring and winter. They call it a “buff.” You can find out more about it at [What Is a Buff and What Does It Do?](/buff-nedir-ne-ise-yarar/).
