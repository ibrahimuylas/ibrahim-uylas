### Where Are the Keramet Village Hot Springs, and How Do You Get There?

Go through Yalova to Orhangazi, then follow the road to İznik. About 17 km after leaving Orhangazi, turn left at the sign for Keramet village. Go through the village to the hot springs.

### Camping at the Keramet Village Hot Springs

##### Available facilities

* In an open area, within a lush green space, a large pool.
* It calms down in the early morning on Saturday.
* Parking
* The initial fee is 3 TL per person (as of April 2014).
* Market (in Keramet Village)
* Clean water
* Toilets
* A café run by the private operator of the hot springs

##### Facilities that are unavailable

* No campsite. You can ask the private operator whether you can pitch a tent.
* Restaurant

Note: This information dates back to 2013.
