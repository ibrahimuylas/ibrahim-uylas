Abant Gölü is one of our most popular locations, boasting numerous natural beauties. Due to its popularity, it gets extremely crowded on weekends. How crowded is it? There’s heavy traffic around the lake. People are emerging from everywhere. However, during the spring months, the camping areas are more peaceful. You can find my thoughts on Abant Gölü with the article “[Is Lake Abant a Good Place to Camp?](/abant-golunde-kamp-yapmali-mi/)"

### Where is Bolu Abant Lake, and how do you get there?

While traveling from İstanbul to Bolu, there is an Abant exit before the Bolu western TEM highway junction. You can easily reach it by following the signs for Abant Lake from the TEM. The entire road is paved. You need to travel on a paved road, approximately 25 km in one direction, after leaving the TEM.

### Bolu Abant Lake camping area information

##### Available facilities

- The national parks are under protection. In the fee-based camping area, a fee is charged per tent. As of 2015, 27 TL is collected.
- The camping area provides kitchen facilities, toilets, and bathrooms. In addition, you can use the electrical outlets in the electrical boxes.
- The entry fee by car is 8 TL.
- Prayer room
- Toilets
- Parking
- Picnic table
- Hotel Accommodation
- Places where you can find food, bread, and water.
- Shops selling gift items.

Note: This information dates back to May 2015.
