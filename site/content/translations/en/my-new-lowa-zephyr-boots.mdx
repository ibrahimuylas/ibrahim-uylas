Here are the new boots I’m getting, Lowa Zephyr Mid Gore Tex. I’m the kind of person who doesn’t buy anything I don’t need, or won’t use. Because I don’t have much money. Even if I did, I wouldn’t buy anything – it just feels like my mindset would always be like that. So, let’s examine this product – why did I buy new boots, and why did I choose Lowa, like, is Lowa really good?

### Why did I buy a new trekking boot?

I asked myself, "What happened to my old boots that led me to decide to buy a new pair?" I think that question is quite important. I’d been a bit rough on my old [Jack Wolfskin Solid Trail boots](/jack-wolfskin-erkek-trekking-botu/). I walked over 500km with them, and I can say that the vast majority of that distance was off-road. I had washed these Gore-Tex boots 2 times in the washing machine using the hand-wash cycle early on. Since then, the Gore Tex property has completely deteriorated. I used the Jack Wolfskin boots for three years, covering over 500km. Naturally, the soles of the boots wore down considerably – more than half of their large teeth had worn away. In addition, there was a slight separation in one part of the boot, and the leather around the ankle area had torn in places. I could have repaired the damaged parts, but the Gore Tex property was no longer present. Despite the soles wearing down, the teeth on them are still functional. Therefore, I continue to use them for dry day trips and at campsites. They're still going strong, actually. But, because I needed a boot with Gore Tex for the Kaçkar mountains, I had to buy a new pair.

Look at the name of these boots, mate, as if they were born in Argentina. I really liked the model; the style is great. It was the first time I was so taken in by a pair. I decided to do a bit of searching to find user reviews right away. Everyone's praised it. There are people saying that as soon as they open the box and put it on, it's, "Uff, what a super boot." "Fantastic thing, it's like it's going to fly me." Come on, mate, I can't believe it. How can you say these things before you've even walked 1 km? It's either advertising or, to get me to watch more, just exaggeration. As I said, I really liked the style, and it's a very good brand, a model that hugs the ankle, with Gore Tex, and it's said to be excellent off-road, and the price is also at a reasonable level compared to competitors.

### Is the LOWA ZEPHYR GTX a good boot?

Let’s get to my opinions. Starting a trip by buying new boots is a risk that could upset you. Before the boots have settled on your feet, they might cause pain or hurt you. So, it’s beneficial to spend a few hours with the boots, even at home. First, let’s talk about the good things. These boots are legendary off-road. Truly legendary. As someone who has worn all sorts of shoes from Salomon, Jack Wolfskin, Columbia, Nike, Mike, and so on, I can say their grip on terrain is legendary. They offer excellent traction on wet and dry rocks, steep terrain, dirt, ditches, and rocky areas. They didn’t slip even once. This really impressed me and they delivered on their promise. One reason is the rubber sole. In addition, the boots are very sturdy and hold their shape well. They grip the ankle nicely and give you the feeling that your feet are safe. You feel like you’re wearing a tank on your feet. The Gore Tex is already a major plus; your feet always stay dry. Now, let’s get to the part that upset me the most. Every time I put on my Jack Wolfskin boots, I think, “Oh, I’ve missed my boots.” The inside and the insole are so comfortable it makes you feel that way. The same effect wasn’t there with the LOWA. They used a thin, shoddy removable insole. Something airy and comfortable for the foot, supposedly, but in reality, it’s not. The bottom of that insole is already the shoe’s sole, so it’s quite hard. This boot doesn’t become bad enough to avoid buying. I’m just saying this because I couldn’t find the comfort of the Jacks (and compared to other brands). We walked 45km in 5 days, and I had no complaints about my feet. The boots provide good insulation from the ground. I think the only downside is the insole not being more comfortable. Maybe I can make them more comfortable by buying a different insole. If I do, I’ll write about it.

### Would I buy the Lowa Zephyr?

It’s purchased solely for performance on the ground. There are longer models, similar to military inflatable boats. The preference is for the model and type. At around 500 TL, this is a fair price for this type of boot. Let’s be clear about this. I believe that the prices for outdoor products are too high, and they are constantly increasing with taxes or fees. I’m saying that 500 TL is a fair price, by comparing with other outdoor products.
