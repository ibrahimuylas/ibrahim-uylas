### Why did I buy a new trekking boot?

I asked myself, "What happened to my old boots that led me to decide to buy a new pair?" I think that question is quite important. I’d been a bit rough on my old [Jack Wolfskin Solid Trail boots](/jack-wolfskin-erkek-trekking-botu/). I walked over 500km with them, and I can say that the vast majority of that distance was off-road. I had washed these Gore-Tex boots 2 times in the washing machine using the hand-wash cycle early on. Since then, the Gore Tex property has completely deteriorated. I used the Jack Wolfskin boots for three years, covering over 500km. Naturally, the soles of the boots wore down considerably – more than half of their large teeth had worn away. In addition, there was a slight separation in one part of the boot, and the leather around the ankle area had torn in places. I could have repaired the damaged parts, but the Gore Tex property was no longer present. Despite the soles wearing down, the teeth on them are still functional. Therefore, I continue to use them for dry day trips and at campsites. They're still going strong, actually. But, because I needed a boot with Gore Tex for the Kaçkar mountains, I had to buy a new pair.

### Is the LOWA ZEPHYR GTX a good boot?

Now, let me share my opinions. Buying a new pair of boots is a risk that could upset someone starting a trip – it could cause pain or hurt your feet before you’ve really gotten used to them. Therefore, it’s beneficial to spend a few hours with boots, even if you’re just at home. Let’s start by talking about the good aspects of these boots. These boots are truly fantastic off-road. As someone who has worn boots from brands like Salamon, Jack Wolfskin, Columbia, and Nike, I can confidently say that their grip on uneven terrain is incredible. I’ve experienced excellent traction on wet and dry rocks, on steep slopes, on dirt, and when crossing ditches and rocky areas. They didn’t slip at all. This really impressed me, and they performed as expected. One reason for this is the use of rubber soles. In addition, the boots are very sturdy and durable. They provide a good grip on the ankle and give you the feeling that your feet are safe. They feel like you’re wearing tank soles. The Gore Tex material is also a major plus – your feet always stay dry. Now, let’s talk about what bothered me the most. When I wear my Jack Wolfskin boots, I always say, “Oh, these boots!” But the soles of these boots weren’t as comfortable as those of the Jack Wolfskin boots. The same effect wasn’t seen with LOWA. They used a rather thin and stiff sole as the inner sole. It felt like a supersonic thing, designed to make your feet comfortable, but in reality, it wasn’t. Because it’s the sole of the shoe, of course. This boot isn’t bad enough to not buy. I’m saying this because I couldn’t find the same comfort as the Jack Wolfskin boots (and compared to other brands). We walked 45km in 5 days, and I had no complaints about my feet. The boots provide excellent insulation from the ground. I think the only downside is that the inner sole could be more comfortable. I might consider buying a different inner sole to make them even more comfortable. I’ll let you know if I do.
