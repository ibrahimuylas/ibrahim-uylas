title: "A Trip I’d Dreamed Of: The Isle of Skye"
description: "I dream of being able to freely roam in every country."
---
I dream of being able to freely roam in every country. That’s why setting out, and being on a journey, represents freedom for me. And for me, a journey begins when I dream of it, and then becomes reality.

The poet said this, and decided to pursue the dream that had been in his mind for a long time.

Driving through the Scottish Highlands, hiking in their mountains, camping, and fully immersing myself in nature have been dreams of mine for the past three years. I don't quite know why, but there was a feeling that I could freely and as I pleased in those lands. My plan for the Isle of Skye was the center of this dream, the heart of it. The idea of going to the summit of Ben Nevis, the highest point in the United Kingdom, and watching the sunrise while gazing at the ocean of clouds filtering through the mountains, was something that made my heart race in my current location.

After three years of dreaming, I’ve finally managed to embark on this wonderful adventure I’ve envisioned. Why did I wait three years? Sometimes the conditions don’t arise, and sometimes your spirit simply doesn’t have the strength to reach for that dream. You just need to wait for the right time. I spent the last 4-5 years building the groundwork to achieve these dreams. So, taking this path today wasn’t easy, but it’s turned out exactly as I planned. I will first imagine, then do whatever is necessary to reach that dream. I will wait patiently until I reach my dreams. My dreams keep me alive, my dreams make me happy, and I live for my dreams.

On 19 June 2021, I set out to realise this dream. Having achieved one dream, I would immediately rush to realise another, and my life would flow like this. There are two distinct reasons why this journey has been special for me. The first is that it marked the beginning of the dreams I had nurtured for years, and it was far more than simply setting out on a journey – it would grant me countless experiences and lessons. The second is that, following the Lycian Way (12 days alone in the mountains in 2014), this would be the longest solo journey I undertook – a four-week journey. At the same time, this was not merely about taking a journey and being on the road; it would be a journey I made to my own world seven years later. I define the Lycian Way in a single word: 'Love'. Because the Lycian Way was love within me. I set out with that journey with immense love, passion, and excitement. The Scottish Highlands, and the Isle of Skye at the heart of that dream, became 'Happiness' for me. Because it was the beginning of the dreams that made me happy, and a wonderful person asked me a question that enabled me to connect all the links in my mind – yes, that question: 'Has the time come to redefine the meaning of happiness?'

The Isle of Skye, the Scottish Highlands, represent for me the beginning of happiness and are, in fact, the embodiment of my dream – they redefined my understanding of what happiness means in my life. For me, the Lycian Road was love, and the Isle of Skye, happiness.
