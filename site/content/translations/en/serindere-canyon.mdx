### Serindere Canyon: Where is it and how do you get there?

After arriving at the Yuvacık dam from Kocaeli, you continue towards the Tepecik village. After passing through Tepecik village, you follow the road leading to the Serindere village on the left. After passing through Serindere village, you will arrive at the place where the stream is located. Immediately after crossing the stream over the bridge, you will find a cafe and a picnic area on the right. After parking your car in a suitable spot and walking past the cafe, you will reach the mill. Then, you will enter the stream and continue upwards into the canyon. If you check using Google maps, you will see an alternative route to take when leaving Yuvacık junction, which is a dirt road, making it easier to travel from Tepecik.

I highly recommend visiting this beautiful canyon, where you can have a very enjoyable time and, at times, barely see the sky.

### Serindere Canyon Camping Area Information

##### Available facilities

- Clean water
- Café
- Picnic Area
- Wc (related to the cafeteria)

##### Facilities that are unavailable

- Camp Site (There isn’t a good place to set up a tent here. But you can request permission to set up in the café’s garden.)
- Grocery shop
- Guesthouse

Note: This information dates back to 2013.

### Serindere Canyon Hiking Routes

#### Serindere Canyon Hike (10.5 km)

- There are no water points on the route. Be sure to refill your water before setting out.
- While completing this route, you will be following the stream. There will be a fork in the road at one point. If you continue on the left, you will reach the waterfall; if you continue on the right, you will reach the canyon.
- This route, which mostly involves moving through the water, will occasionally require you to step onto the ground.
- When you enter the canyon, you will realize that you see very little of the sky.
- The water level will vary depending on the season you are in. If you are visiting in the spring, remember that the water level could reach up to your chest.
- Watch out for slippery rocks and stones covered in moss in the canyon.

- There is a fountain on the road, but you should still take water from the İnönü plateau and set off.
- The trail, which is proceeding on a forest road, will initially involve a mix of uphill and downhill sections, before transitioning to a downhill route for the final 4km.
- A moderately difficult, long course. Its length can be challenging.
- You can reach İnönü Valley by starting from Serindere, using the same route.

- To avoid water shortages during the route, make sure to fill up your water supply before you set out. Or, you can obtain water from the cafe at the destination.
- The route begins in Serindere, continuing along the river, and completing the canyon. Afterwards, you enter a forest dirt path, passing through Sultaniye, and returning to the Serindere canyon’s starting point, which is the location of the mill.
- This is a medium-difficulty course. It can be tiring due to its length.
- We will be walking through a river and then through the forest, so you should have spare shoes.
- It would be beneficial to have a helmet and protective gear.
