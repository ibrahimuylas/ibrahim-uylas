### Where is Olgunlar Highland and how do you get there?

Starting from Yusufeli, the route follows the path to Altıparmak (Barhal) village. The road then ascends, and this road, which is mostly unpaved, becomes increasingly deteriorated. You first reach the village of Yaylalar, and then Olgunlar Highland. The road continues smoothly and paved to Altıparmak village. However, between Altıparmak and Yaylalar village, there is a rough dirt road. You can travel quite slowly with your vehicle. The road between Yaylalar village and Olgunlar Highland is a 3km concrete road.

### Camping at Olgunlar Highland

##### Available facilities

- Camp Site (You can set up your tent in the flat areas you find. There is no private business.)
- Clean water
- Toilets
- Prayer room
- There are three guesthouses in the highland village (Kaçkar Pansiyon, Olgunlar Pansiyon, and Denizgölü Pansiyon).
- There are two grocery shops, a kiosk selling things like gözleme and ayran, and a café.
- One can hike from the Olgunlar valleys to the peak of the Kaçkar mountains, or reach Yukarı Kavrun Highland by traversing the mountain.

##### Facilities that are unavailable

- Especially during holidays or festivals, there is a significant increase in traffic in the area. As a result, you may experience difficulty finding a parking space for your car.
- Due to severe winter weather conditions, no one remains in the meadow.

Note: This information dates back to August 2013.

### Olgunlar Highland Hiking Routes

#### From Olgunlar Highland via Dibe Düzü and Naletleme to Büyük Deniz Lake (11.5 km)

- Looking upwards from Olgunlar yaylas, and following the trail heading to the right within the valley, you will reach Dibe Düzü yaylas, where there are now only ruined houses. You may encounter local residents who have come to the area for livestock. Then, continuing to follow the trail, you will ascend to the right, and when you see the ground end and rocks begin, you will realize you have reached Naletleme geçidi. At Naletleme geçidi, you may sometimes lose sight of the trail, especially when it is foggy. Therefore, you should follow every approximately 15 meters, looking for the “babaları” – a series of stacked stones. After overcoming this challenging pass, you will descend, and this trail will lead you to Büyük Deniz gölü (Big Sea Lake).
- Specifically, the Naletleme Pass will be exhausting for you. You’ll realize the benefit of wearing your trekking boots when you don’t feel the stones under your feet.
- A moderately challenging, yet beautiful course. The difficulty of your walk may increase depending on the weather conditions.
- Don’t forget that the weather can change dramatically when you go above 3000 meters. You could suddenly find yourself enveloped in fog, or experience a sudden downpour.
- You won’t have any water problems while hiking. You can drink from the flowing streams – you really won’t be able to get enough of the taste. The water flowing at this altitude is always clean. You should be careful not to pollute it.

- Follow the path straight uphill from Olgunlar Highland to Hastaf Highland. Continue to Dilberdüzü and then Deniz Lake. From there the route crosses rocky ground to the summit in the Kaçkar Mountains.

- Starting from Dilberdüzü, this route initially takes you to Hastaf valley, and then to Olgunlar Highland. From Olgunlar Highland, you will then travel by road to Yaylalar village. Once you enter the valley by going left from Yaylalar village, this trail will lead you to Satelef Highland. The 10km section of the road from here will be uphill. The next 10 km will then descend.
- This is a challenging course. The ascent and descent between Yaylalar village and Satelef Highland, as well as the transitions through the mountain, can be tiring. Depending on the weather conditions, the hike may become even more difficult.
- Don’t forget that the weather can change dramatically when you go above 3000 meters. You could suddenly find yourself enveloped in fog, or experience a sudden downpour.

- The route starts at Satelef Highland. You gain about 900 metres in 4.5 km to reach the pass. From there you descend a steep 700 metres over 2 km into the valley, then reach Olgunlar Highland after another 2 km.
- After Geçitten, a 2km descent may be challenging. You should be careful here.
- This is a challenging course. The conditions can make your hike even more difficult.
- Don’t forget that the weather can change dramatically when you go above 3000 meters. You could suddenly find yourself enveloped in fog, or experience a sudden downpour.
