## Kabak Bay to Sidyma: the climb to Alınca and the Yediburunlar crossing

This section summarizes the second stage, which begins 8 km from Kabak Bay and reaches Sidyma Ancient City via the Yediburunlar area, covering a distance of 23 km. A long climb and the historical remains in Sidyma are the defining characteristics of this section.

> Historical field record: The following practical information is based on the experience of 21 September 2014. Water, facility, access, and camping conditions should not be considered current information.

### Stage summary

- **Stage:** 2 of 11
- **Start and finish:** Kabak Bay – Sidyma Ancient City
- **Waypoints:** Alınca, Yediburunlar and Boğaziçi village
- **Distance:** 23 km based on signposted distances
- **Hiking time:** The total duration is not recorded; it is stated that the 8 km hike from Kabak Bay to Alınca took 3.5 hours.
- **Walked:** 21 September 2014
- **Current conditions:** No recent verification was available for this guide.

### Terrain, difficulty and navigation

According to the 2014 journal, the stage starts with a steep, tiring climb from Kabak Bay to Alınca, then gets easier. The trail markings looked sparse on the approach to Sidyma, and I checked the route on the map twice.

### Water and food — 2014 record

In Kabak Bay, Alınca, and Sidyma, it was noted that water is available; there’s a market in a village located approximately 3 km from Kabak and Sidyma. The log also mentions that a breakfast was eaten in a house after the Alınca departure.

### Accommodation, camping and ways off the route — 2014 record

In Alınca, a guesthouse is mentioned, while a guesthouse was not recorded in Sidyma. The author stated that he spent the night in a tent set up in the garden of a house in Sidyma, at the invitation of the homeowner. The check-in/check-out options within Etap (presumably a booking platform) were not recorded.

### Notable places

The stops included in this stage are: Alınca (a village), the view of Yediburunlar, Boğaziçi village, and the ruins from the Lycian, Roman, and Byzantine periods of Sidyma.

### What I learned on this stage

The log entry for the second day highlights the importance of setting aside specific time for the long ascent at the beginning of the stage: the hike from Kabak–Alınca only took 3.5 hours and was the most strenuous part of the day.

- There’s a steep 8km ascent from Kabak to Alınca. It took 3.5 hours and was quite exhausting.
- After the steep descent, the path becomes easier, and you reach Sidyma via Alınca.
- I set up my tent in the garden of a house in Sidyma (at the invitation of the owner) and spent the night there.
- Shops: at Kabak Bay and in a village 3 km before Sidyma
- You can get water at Kabak Bay, Alınca and Sidyma.
- There’s a guesthouse in Alınca, but not in Sidyma.
- In Sidyma, there isn't a cafe, a market, or a guesthouse.
