Pürenli Highland, located within the borders of Bolu, is at an altitude of approximately 1400 metres. It has a large and beautiful forest. Where the highland houses are, there is a small pond formed by the melting snow. Youth camps and highland festivals are held during certain periods in the summer. Between November and May, there is a high chance of seeing snow. When coming from İstanbul via the TEM motorway, you should exit at the Düzce junction and continue towards Güzeldere. I can't remember if there were signposts on the road, but you can reach the highland by following the route below.

### Pürenli Highland camping area information

##### Available facilities

- When arriving from Düzce, a large area will greet you. There is no specific facility for camping.
- If you’re here during a busy season, you can set up your tent in a suitable spot like this.
- If you are in a calm period, it would be more enjoyable to set up your tent by the lake, in the area with the cottages on the highland, after passing the fountain a bit further ahead.
- This area has a very large forest, and you can easily find plenty of firewood for a fire.
- There’s a fountain in the meadow, but there are no toilets. The toilets at the houses in the meadow exist, but they are closed and locked. Besides, it wouldn’t be right to enter someone else’s property without permission. You should use the forest for the toilets.
- In April, May, October, and November, the highland will be colder. You should dress warmly.

##### Facilities that are unavailable

- Of course, things like restaurants, hotels, and guesthouses aren’t available.
- There isn’t really a business like that, or anything like a market.

Note: This information dates back to November 2014.
