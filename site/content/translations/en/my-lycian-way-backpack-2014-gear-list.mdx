This list records the tent, mat, clothing, first aid, and other backpack equipment I chose to use when hiking the Lycian Way on September 20, 2014, as part of my efforts to reduce my weight.

## 2014 backpack list

Okay, how did I prepare my bag when I embarked on my Lycian journey on September 20, 2014? 

The Lycian Trail is a long route. In fact, it’s the longest trail in Turkey. I’ve created a 21-day plan. You need to carefully measure even small increments (grams) when undertaking long walks; even a slight excess of 10 grams can become increasingly heavy as time goes on. Therefore, I’ve carefully measured and woven it together.

* First I replaced my tent. I’d already been planning to buy a good one. I looked for a lightweight tent I’d be comfortable with on the Lycian Way and settled on one of the lightest tents in the world, the Ferrino Light-Tent 2. [Read more about the tent here](/ferrino-lightent-2-kamp-cadiri/).
* Getting a comfortable night’s sleep was very important. Since regular military mats only provided insulation, you could wake up every morning with aches all over. Because I’ll be camping in a tent for most of my trip, the mat became even more important. I decided to get an inflatable mat – specifically, the Thermarest X-Term L model. I did this because it’s the best for the job. Then, I looked for other inflatable mats that would also provide good insulation and inflate quite a bit, so I bought the Ferrino X-Tech 3.8cm inflatable mat. [You can watch the product review here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xyDx1ZmiHdM)
* During the time I was traveling, the chance of rain was low. However, I still decided not to take any risks and brought along lightweight, space-saving disposable raincoats.
* I packed just 1 change of clothes. I thought I could alternate outfits, wash them when I found water and buy more when I reached civilisation. That’s why I figured 1 spare outfit was enough.
* During long walks, there can be pulling in the knees due to the strain. That’s why I brought one pair of knee supports with me.
* Pen and a notebook to write down my daily memories.
* I bought a camera, a monopod, and a small tripod.
* Considering the idea that it might be needed in the darkness, and with the hope of easily finding a pen-powered flashlight, I bought one.
* I was using a tablet as my phone. The large screen was an advantage. Although it was a bit heavy, I preferred to carry it with me.
* I grabbed a small knife and my pipe, just in case I might need them. I’m a man who enjoys a little pleasure. My pipe will be my companion.

When calculating the weights, the following results were obtained;

* Bag: 2100gr
* Tent: 1700gr
* Inflatable Mat: 1450gr
* Sleep Mask: 1400gr
* Tablet : 580gr
* Clothing: 300gr
* Pen + Notebook : 250gr
* Dizlik + Raincoat: 250gr
* Biscuit: 200gr
* Monopod + Tripod: 180gr
* Knife + Gloves: 150gr
* Fener: 150gr
* Other miscellaneous: 300gr
* Water: 2000gr

It appears to be a total of 11 kg. It’s a very ideal weight.

 

I didn’t understand what was happening, and when starting, the bag weight, excluding water, was 11kg. I needed to carry at least 3 liters of water, and I walked with an average of 13-14kg.
