This beautiful beach, located between the districts of Kalkan and Kaş in Antalya, at the point where the Kaputaş canyon reaches the sea, is definitely worth a visit. To reach Kaputaş beach, you need to descend 187 steps from the road, and then climb back up. In my opinion, it’s worth it, and it also has a crystal-clear sea.

### Where is Kaputaş Beach and how do you get there?

The location is found on the roadside between Kaş and Kalkan. It’s closer to Kalkan. If you are travelling by your own vehicle, you can follow this road. The schedules for dolmuşes (shared taxis) travelling from Kaş, Kalkan, and Patara are as follows.

### Kaputaş Beach camping area information

##### Available facilities

* The municipality of Kaş operates a café.

  * WC, shower
  * Lounge chair, umbrella (The price was 5+5 TL in September 2016.)
  * Café
* There is parking available. So, there are empty spaces along the road. You can park your vehicle there.

##### Facilities that are unavailable

* There is no camping area. There isn't a designated area on the beach where you can set up a tent. You can camp at the campsites in Kaş or Patara, or you can set up a tent in a spot along the Lycian Way.
* Of course, you won’t find restaurants, hotels, or guesthouses, but you could consider places like Kaş or Kalkan.

Note: This information dates back to September 2016.

### Kaputaş Beach Hiking Routes
