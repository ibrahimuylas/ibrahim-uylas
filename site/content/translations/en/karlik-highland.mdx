### Where is Karlık Highland and how do you get there?

Go via Yalova to Çınarcık. Take the Teşvikiye road past Erikli Highland, then Erikli Waterfall and Dipsiz Lake. At Delmece Highland, take the dirt road to the right and follow it for about 3 km to Karlık Highland. It’s rough, so go slowly. In summer there’s a broad meadow here and in spring you’ll see a lake. That’s what makes this highland so interesting. You can even see the lake on Google Maps :)

### Camping at Karlık Highland

##### Available facilities

- You can pitch your tent on the wide, flat ground at Karlık Highland.
- Clean water

##### Facilities that are unavailable

- Groceries (the last shop is in Teşvikiye village).
- Guesthouse
- Cafeteria. The closest one is in Delmece Highland. The next one is at Erikli Waterfall.
- Toilets

Note: This information dates back to 2014.

### Hiking routes at Karlık Highland

#### From Esenköy across the Karlık and Delmece highlands, then through Selimiye village to Narlı (32 km)

- The route begins in Esenköy, progressing through a beautiful landscape before entering the forest. The path ascends approximately 11 kilometers and 800 meters through the forest to reach Karlık Highland, which may be tiring. From this point, the route descends to reach Karlık Highland, followed by Delmece Highland and then Selimiye village. After Delmece Highland, you will follow the asphalt road, occasionally crossing slopes until you reach Narlı at the end of the route.
- There are places to get water along the road.
- This is a medium-difficulty course. Its length, and the initial ascent to Karlık Highland (Karlık Highland), can be tiring.
- You can also do it using the same route, starting from Narlı.

#### Starting from Delmece Highland, passing through Karlık and Aldere highlands, and reaching Hayriye village (18 km).

- The route begins in Delmece Highland, continuing through the forest and reaching Karlık Highland. Afterwards, you will proceed along an unpaved forest path, arriving at Aldere Highland. This section of the path is downhill. Subsequently, you will continue along an unpaved forest path, reaching Hayriye village.
- There are places to get water along the road.
- You can also do it starting from Hayriye village.
- Medium difficulty course.

#### From Esenköy to Kocadere via the Karlık and Delmece highlands (30 km)

- The trail begins by ascending from Kocadere, progressing alongside a beautiful view before entering the forest. Passing by the Dipsiz Lake, you will descend to reach Delmece Highland. Afterwards, you will follow a forest dirt path to arrive at Karlık Highland, then descend to reach Esenköy. As you approach Esenköy, a magnificent view will greet you.
- There are places to get water along the road.
- You can also do it using the same route, starting from Esenköy.
- This is a medium-difficulty course. The 30km distance can be tiring.

- Medium difficulty course.
