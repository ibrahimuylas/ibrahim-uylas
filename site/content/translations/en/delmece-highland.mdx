### Delmece Valley: Where is it and how do you get there?

Starting from Yalova, one first reaches Çınarcık. Following the Teşvikiye road, one travels through to Erikli Yaylası, then passes by the Erikli waterfall and the Dipsiz lake, reaching Delmece valley. There’s a beautiful, paved road through the forest.

### Delmece Valley campsite information

##### Available facilities

- You can set up tents in the wide plains of Delmece village.
- Mesict
- Clean water
- Toilets
- Cafeteria (There’s one cafeteria run by a local resident from the area. It’s closed outside of the summer season.)

##### Facilities that are unavailable

- Market (at the latest market in Teşvikiye village)
- Guesthouse

Note: This information dates back to 2014.

### Delmece valley hiking routes

#### From the Delmece valley, to the Erikli waterfall, and then on to the Teşvikiye village (12.4 km).

- The route begins in Delmece valley and, after proceeding a short distance along a dirt road, reaches Erikli waterfall. Then, the route continues through the forest, extending as far as the entrance to Teşvikiye village.
- There are water points along the road.
- Medium difficulty course.
- You can also do it starting from Teşvikiye.

#### From Erikli waterfall to Delmece valley (9.5 km)

  scrolling='no'

- The trail begins at the Erikli waterfall, following the stream it flows into, and leads through the forest to Delmece Valley.
- Take the water from Erikli waterfall before leaving.
- Medium difficulty course.
- You can also do the same route starting at Delmece Highland. It will be easier in this direction because you’ll be going downhill.

#### From Selimiye village, in the Delmece valley, and then on to Erikli Waterfall (18 km)

- Turn left into the forest at the end of Selimiye village to pick up the trail. It reaches Delmece Highland after about 7 km. Follow the dirt road past Dipsiz Lake and on to Erikli Waterfall.
- Do not take your water before leaving Selimiye village. Afterwards, there is drinking water available in Delmece valley and at the Erikli waterfall.
- Medium difficulty course.
- You can also do it using the same route, starting from the Erikli Waterfall.

#### From Esenköy across the Karlık and Delmece highlands, then through Selimiye village to Narlı (32 km)

  width='100%'

  style={{ borderRadius: '10px 10px' }}

- The route begins in Esenköy, progressing through a beautiful landscape before entering the forest. The path ascends approximately 11 kilometers and 800 meters through the forest to reach Karlık Plateau, which may be tiring. From this point, the route descends to reach Karlık Plateau, followed by Delmece Plateau and then Selimiye village. After Delmece Plateau, you will follow the asphalt road, occasionally crossing slopes until you reach Narlı at the end of the route.
- There are places to get water along the road.
- This is a medium-difficulty course. Its length, and the initial ascent to Karlık Yaylası (Karlık Valley), can be tiring.
- You can also do it using the same route, starting from Narlı.

#### Starting from Delmece valley, passing through Karlık and Aldere valleys, and reaching Hayriye village (18 km).

- The route begins in Delmece Valley, continuing through the forest and reaching Karlık Valley. Afterwards, you will proceed along an unpaved forest path, arriving at Aldere Valley. This section of the path is downhill. Subsequently, you will continue along an unpaved forest path, reaching Hayriye village.
- There are places to get water along the road.
- You can also do it starting from Hayriye village.
- Medium difficulty course.

#### From Esenköy to Kocadere via the Karlık and Delmece highlands (30 km)

- The trail begins in Kocadere, ascending and then continues through a forest. You will pass by the “Dipsiz göl” (Bottomless Lake) and descend to reach Delmece Valley. Afterwards, you will proceed through a forest dirt path to arrive at Karlık Valley, and then descend to reach Esenköy. Upon approaching Esenköy, a magnificent view will greet you.
- There are places to get water along the road.
- You can also do it using the same route, starting from Esenköy.
- This is a medium-difficulty course. The 30km distance can be tiring.

#### Starting from the Delmece valley, proceeding to the Aldere valley, and returning to the Delmece valley (23 km).

- The trail begins in the forest, in the middle of Delmece Valley, and continues through the forest, heading towards Selimiye village. Before reaching the road to Selimiye, it continues towards Aldere Valley again, following a dirt path through the forest, extending back to Delmece Valley.
- The course is one where you will first ascend up to approximately 100 meters, and then transition to a descent. After Aldere Valley, you will ascend again.
- There are places to get water along the road.
- This is a medium-difficulty course. The 23km distance can be tiring.

#### From Selimiye village to Karlık Highland, then on to Delmece Highland (13.5 km)

- The route begins at a fork in the road just outside Selimiye village, proceeding on a dirt path through the forest and ascending to Karlık Valley. Afterwards, it continues through the forest, leading to Delmece Valley.
- Medium difficulty course.
- There are places to get water along the road.

#### From Selimiye village to Delmece Highland, then on to Erikli Highland (17.5 km)

- The route begins in Selimiye village, proceeding through the forest and eventually reaching Delmece Valley. Afterwards, it continues through the forest, passing the Erikli Waterfall, and concludes in Erikli Valley.
- Medium difficulty course.
- There are places to get water along the road.
