One of İstanbul’s most beloved natural areas is Belgrade Forest. It’s a convenient and beautiful option for İstanbul’s nature lovers, offering walking, cycling, and running trails.

### Where Is Belgrad Forest, and How Do You Get There?

While traveling from İstanbul in the direction of Sarıyer, you simply need to follow the signs for Bahçeköy. The subsequent signs will easily guide you to the Belgrad forest.

### Camping in Belgrad Forest

##### Available facilities

The national parks are under protection. A camping area has not been designated. You can set up tents in suitable locations after walking into the forest. Please be aware that if someone makes a complaint or the officials arrive, problems could arise.

- Walking trails, cycling routes, and running paths are available.
- The initial fee is 12 TL by car, and 8 TL by motorcycle.
- Prayer room
- Toilets
- Parking
- Picnic table
- A kiosk for water, chocolate and similar supplies.
- It gets busy in summer. Early spring and late autumn are better times to visit.

Note: This information dates back to February 2016.
