### Where is Menekşe Highland and how do you get there?

You can reach Menekşe Highland by two different routes. The first is: Kocaeli -> Yuvacık to Aytepe, and then following the signs for Menekşe Highland to reach the valley. The second route is: Kocaeli -> Gölcük -> Nüzhetiye, again following the signs for Menekşe Highland to reach the valley. The first route transitions to a dirt road after Aytepe, and you may need to drive slowly at times to avoid scraping your car. The second route is smoother than the first, transitioning to a dirt road after Nüzhetiye.

### Menekşe Highland Camping Area Information

##### Available facilities

- Camp Site (You can set up your tent in the flat areas you find. There is no private business.)
- Clean water
- Toilets

##### Facilities that are unavailable

- Grocery shop
- Restaurant
- Café

Note: This information dates back to 2013.

### Menekşe Highland Hiking Routes

#### From Aytepe and Soğukpınar via Papaz Çayırı to Menekşe Highland (11.5 km)

- The route begins in Aytepe, and first you descend to Soğukpınar. From there, you then follow a steep, forest trail to Papaz Çayır and then on to Menekşe Highland. Afterwards, you follow the same path back to your starting point.
- There is water in Soğukpınar, Papaz Çayırı, and Menekşe Highland.
- The forest trail between Soğukpınar and Kayaklıklar might be a bit tiring for you. However, it’s generally a moderate route for beginners and an easy one for experienced hikers.

#### From Aytepe and Soğukpınar via Papaz Çayırı and Menekşe Highland to Servetiye Karşı village (22 km)

- Starting on the route from Aytepe, you first descend to Soğukpınar. From there, you then follow a steep, forest trail to Papaz Çayır and then on to Menekşe Highland. Finally, you descend via a forest path to Servetiye Karşı village.
- There is water in Soğukpınar, Papaz Çayırı, and Menekşe Highland.
- The forest trail between Soğukpınar and Kayaklıklar might be a bit tiring, as it’s a medium-level route and quite long.

- From Kırıntı village, follow the dirt road to Menekşe Highland and then Papaz Çayırı. From there, a forest path takes you to Soğukpınar.
- In the villages of Kırıntı, Soğukpınar, Papaz Çayırı, and Menekşe Highland, there is water.
- Overall, it’s a relatively easy course.

- The route begins in Kırıntı village, and proceeds along a dirt road to the Menekşe-Subatımı Highland, passing by the Menekşe Highland, reaching the summit and then returning along the same path.
- The viewpoint offers a beautiful panorama.
- There’s water in Kırıntı village and on Menekşe Highland.
- Overall, it’s a relatively easy course.

#### From Aytepe and Soğukpınar via Menekşe Highland to Karaaslan campsite (20 km)

- You begin the route at Aytepe, following a steep, forest trail to Menekşe Highland. Then, you retrace the same path, reaching Servetiye Village, descending from Soğukpınar, and subsequently arriving at Karaaslan camping area.
- There are water points on the road.
- A moderate course. Its length can be tiring.
