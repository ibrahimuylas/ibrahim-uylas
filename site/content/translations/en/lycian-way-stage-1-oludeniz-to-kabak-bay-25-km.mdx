## Ölüdeniz to Kabak Bay: from Babadağ to the coast

This page describes the first 25 km of my 2014 walk, from Ölüdeniz via Faralya to Kabak Bay. The climb around Babadağ, the Butterfly Valley junction and my overnight stop at Kabak were the main features of this stage.

> Historical field record: The following practical information is based on the experience of 20 September 2014. Water, facility, access, and camping conditions should not be considered current information.

### Stage summary

- **Stage:** 1 of 11
- **Start and finish:** Ölüdeniz – Kabak Bay
- **Waypoints:** Faralya and the Butterfly Valley junction
- **Distance:** 25 km based on signposted distances
- **Walking time:** The total walking time is unknown. My journal records that I reached Kabak Bay at about 17:00.
- **Walked:** 20 September 2014
- **Current conditions:** No recent verification was available for this guide.

### Terrain, difficulty and navigation

In my 2014 notes, the route climbs around Babadağ, then follows trails between settlements down to the coast. I skipped the descent into Butterfly Valley because of time and how I was feeling. I noted a steep, dangerous passage on the detour where people used ropes.

### Water and food — 2014 record

It has been noted that water can be found in villages along the route; there’s a cafe and market in Faralya, and a market in Kabak Bay. However, the current status of these has not been verified.

### Accommodation, camping and ways off the route — 2014 record

I recorded guesthouses in Faralya and paid campsites in Butterfly Valley and at Kabak Bay. After I was told not to pitch a tent at Kabak, I spent the night on the beach in my sleeping bag. That account does not establish current permission to camp there. My notes did not identify any exit points partway through this stage.

### Notable places

The view of Ölüdeniz, the area around Babadağ, the distinction between Faralya, the Butterfly Valley, and Kabak Bay were all noted during this stage.

### What I learned on this stage

The initial log reveals that adding additional goals, such as a descent into Butterfly Valley outside the planned route, altered the time and effort required. The author had abandoned the Butterfly Valley descent, considering their current health and time conditions.

- The view of Ölüdeniz while climbing Babadağ was truly beautiful.
- I didn’t descend into Butterfly Valley because I was short on time. Allow about 3 hours for the descent and climb back.
- There’s a steep and dangerous area down into the Butterfly Valley, which is descended using ropes. Caution is required.
- I didn’t feel particularly thirsty in this section. There are villages along the route, and access to water is very good.
- I spent the night sleeping in a sleeping bag on the beach at Kabak Bay.
- In Faralya, there are many guesthouses and cafes. There’s also a market.
- There are also businesses where you can set up a tent in the Butterfly Valley.
- There's a market in Kabak Bay.
- There’s a guard at Kabak Bay who says setting up tents is prohibited.
- You can set up a tent in the secluded businesses in Kabak Bay, or by walking 10 minutes into the forest in the direction of Alınca, in a wide open area.

I had calculated the route length based on the values on the signs. However, since the drawing on Wikiloc is based on a map, there are differences.
