## Quick summary

- **430 km:** The historical single-track GPS record from Ölüdeniz to Hisarçandır
- **230 km:** The overall distance I recorded in my journal for the Ölüdeniz–Kaleüçağız walk, 20–30 September 2014
- **11 stages:** Each stage below links to a route guide and a daily journal

## I walked a 230 km route in 11 days.

## Ölüdeniz–Hisarçandır 430 km route record

> Historical map note: The route descriptions and record in this section relate to preparations I made in September 2014, and should not be used as current passage or service information.

The Lycian Way runs from Fethiye to Antalya. According to my 2014 notes, you could start in Ölüdeniz or walk the other way from Hisarçandır. The route occasionally followed roads but mostly kept to trails with red-and-white markers. I studied each stage separately because difficulty, water, accommodation and food varied along the way.
