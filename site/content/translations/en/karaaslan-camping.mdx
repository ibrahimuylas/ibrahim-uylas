### Karaaslan camping: Where is it and how do you get there?

From Yuvacık in Kocaeli, follow the signs for Karaaslan Kamping. The paved road runs beside the reservoir. It’s a lovely drive, and the campsite is just beyond the far end.

### Karaaslan camping information

##### Available facilities

- Camp Site (no tent fees)
- Clean water
- Toilets
- Prayer room
- Restaurant
- Café
- Grocery shop
- Paintball
- ATV
- Nargile Cafe
- Children’s Play Park
- Volleyball/Basketball Court
- Guesthouse
- http://www.karaaslanalabalik.com/

Note: This information dates back to 2014.

### Karaaslan camping hiking routes

- Starting at Rotaya Karaaslan Kamping, you begin with a steep ascent of approximately 500 meters, followed by a descent, which leads you to Yuvacık.
- The route consists of an unpaved track through the forest.
- There are water points on the road.
- It’s an easy course, but its length can be tiring.

#### From Aytepe and Soğukpınar via Menekşe Highland to Karaaslan campsite (20 km)

- You begin the route at Aytepe, following a steep, forest trail to Menekşe Highland. Then, you retrace the same path, reaching Servetiye Village, descending from Soğukpınar, and subsequently arriving at Karaaslan camping area.
- There are water points on the road.
- A moderate course. Its length can be tiring.

#### From Yuvacık Dam to Aytepe, then to Lake İznik via Papaz Çayırı (62 km)

- There are numerous water points along the trail.
- Starting from the Yuvacık junction, you walk along the side of the road to reach Karaaslan camping. Afterwards, you continue along the same path to Servetiye Village, and then to Soğukpınar in Aytepe, via an old forest path. From there, you proceed uphill to Papaz Çayırı and then to Sansarak village. After passing through Sansarak village, you will reach İznik.
- This is a medium-difficulty course. The length of the route may be challenging. You may prefer to complete it in sections, rather than all at once.
