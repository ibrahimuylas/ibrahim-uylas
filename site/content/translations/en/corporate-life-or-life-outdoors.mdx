---
title: "Corporate Life or Life Outdoors?"
description: "Could I really swap my desk for a house in the mountains? And do I even have to choose between the two?"
---
I often ask myself this question. On one hand, there’s a corporate life; on the other, the constant longing for nature. Could I live in a mountain retreat, or could I continue my life as an office worker in the city (until the end)?

I really value a structured life with a regular income and a stable way of living. I appreciate that your job is defined, your earnings are clear, your monthly expenses are known, and the lifestyle they provide is secure. And, of course, all of this makes me happy and content. However, I can’t imagine continuing a corporate life and simultaneously managing household tasks – I can’t picture myself working at a desk and constantly juggling housework until retirement. I simply can’t see myself doing that. Instead, I dream of abandoning the corporate world and living in a remote mountain dwelling, tending to my garden, immersing myself in nature, and simply enjoying a quiet, peaceful, and serene life. But, I can’t envision that either. I can’t imagine leaving the corporate world, nor can I imagine embracing a natural one. I don’t believe there’s a necessity to make a choice; someone can enjoy both. That’s what I do – I spend my weekends walking 40km, driving long distances, and then sleeping at night, only to return to my computer at 8 am on Monday and face the challenges of my work life.

The reality is, the challenge lies in living two lives simultaneously. On one hand, struggling with the difficulties of a career, and on the other, continuing responsibilities like loans, rent, and debts – while also finding time to travel, pursue hobbies, and document it all. And, all of this while trying to maintain motivation at its highest level. Yes, truly, this is a difficult thing. You can do it too, if you can. You don’t have to choose just one life.
