## Üzümlü to Bezirgan: Delik Kemer and the rocky crossing near Kalkan

This guide covers the 22 km sixth stage from Üzümlü via Akbel and Delik Kemer to Kalkan and on towards Bezirgan. The 2014 account describes it as considerably harder than the previous 2 days because of the lack of water, overgrown paths and rocky crossings.

> Historical field record: The following practical information is based on the experience of 25 September 2014. Water, facility, access, and camping conditions should not be considered current information.

### Stage summary

- **Stage:** 6 of 11
- **Start and finish:** Üzümlü – Bezirgan
- **Waypoints:** Akbel, Delik Kemer and Kalkan
- **Distance:** 22 km based on signposted distances
- **Walking time:** The total walking time was not recorded.
- **Walked:** 25 September 2014
- **Current conditions:** No recent verification was available for this guide.

### Terrain, difficulty and navigation

According to notes from 2014, most of the course consists of trails. In the Delik Kemer approach, the trails are surrounded by vegetation, followed by rocky sections requiring careful and balanced passage. It is particularly noted that finding direction at night will be difficult, and that the ground surface is sparse.

### Water and food — 2014 record

It was noted that there was no water available from Akbel to Kalkan. The author recorded at least 3 liters of water transport for this historical experience and described a section where less than half a liter of water was available. Water and logistical support were obtained in Kalkan. The current location and status of the sources were not verified.

### Accommodation, camping and ways off the route — 2014 record

It was recorded that a wide, but rocky area, suitable for a flat camping ground, was chosen, approximately 2 km from Bezirgan, for the night, on the path. Regular accommodation and on-the-go exit options were not determined.

### Notable places

Akbel, the historic water structure – the “Delik Kemer,” “Kalkan,” and “Bezirgan” plains – are part of the stops recorded for this stage.

### What I learned on this stage

The journal indicates that the sixth day’s log should consider both the water plan and the light plan together. The author had stopped a 18:00 walk in order to be able to set up camp on a rocky surface, having remained dehydrated and avoided complete darkness.

- The section from Delik Kemer to Bezirgan, which is approximately 12km.
- After Akbel, there is no water until Kalkan. You should have at least 3 liters of water with you.
- I walked from Delik Kemer to Kalkan on less than half a litre of water.

  - Despite being dehydrated, there are passages with steep rock formations. Caution should be exercised.
  - We are proceeding on the path. It is absolutely essential not to enter at night.

- I spent the night setting up a large area, 2km from Bezirgan, with tents.
- This section is more challenging than the others.
- There is a water shortage between Akbel and Kalkan. It should be avoided with at least 3 liters.
- The trail consists mostly of unpaved paths. Finding a suitable place to set up a tent can be quite difficult.
