### Where is Yukarı Kavrun Highland, and how do you get there?

You can reach the highland on a minibus from Ayder. Don’t take your own car onto this road unless it’s a 4×4 or has good ground clearance. The road deteriorates about 2 km out of Ayder and you can scrape the underside of your car.

### Yukarı Kavrun Highland camping area information

##### Available facilities

- Camping Area (You can set up a tent in the back of any pension by paying their fees, or in any spot you find after a little while past the highland homes.)
- Guesthouse (Prices shoot up on public holidays. When we stayed over Eid at the end of Ramadan, we paid 45 TL per person per night, including breakfast.)
- Clean water
- Toilets
- Cafeterias (where you can enjoy your tea/coffee, eat, and play okey/tavla).

##### Facilities that are unavailable

- The market (there are a couple of small places where you can find basic necessities).
- Restaurant

Note: This information dates back to August 2017.

- The final 5km of the route, starting from Yukarı Kavrun Highland, involves a strenuous climb of 1300 meters, making it a demanding trek.
- Specifically, attention should be paid to the rocks during the peak of the climb.

#### Yukarı Kavrun Highland, Mezovit, Büyük Deniz lake (12.3 km)

- There are water points along the route. Be careful not to contaminate the water by drinking from the stream.
- After traveling approximately 2.5 km upwards from Yukarı Kavrun Highland, you enter the valley to the left. You will continue upwards, with the summit in front of you. After passing Öküzyatağı lake, you will reach Mezovit. Following the route, you will then reach Meterez lake and subsequently Büyükdeniz lake.
- From Meterez Lake it’s downhill to the highland pasture, which makes the return walk easier.
