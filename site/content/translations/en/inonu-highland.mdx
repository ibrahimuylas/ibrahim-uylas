### Where is İnönü Valley, and how do you get there?

After arriving in Kocaeli, you continue from the Yuvacık fork towards the Yuvacık dam. When you go upwards along the dam’s right side and reach the highest point, you will see the sign for Tepecik Village and İnönü Valley at the fork in the road. Following this road, after passing through Tepecik Village, you will reach İnönü Valley by following the road going upwards to the right from the mosque.

### Inönü Plateau Camping Area Information

##### Available facilities

- Camp Site (There is no specific business/establishment. You can set up a tent anywhere in the meadow.)
- Clean water
- WC (Because it’s not a private business, don’t expect excessive hygiene.)
- Volleyball Court

##### Facilities that are unavailable

- The grocery store/market needs to be handled in Yuvacık.
- Restaurant
- Café
- Guesthouse

Note: This information dates back to 2013.

### İnönü Valley Hiking Routes

#### From İnönü plateau to Kayaüstü plateau, and then on to Tepecik village (10.5 km).

- You should set off on your way, taking water from the İnönü plateau.
- Using forest tracks and tractor paths, you will first reach Kaya Üstü Valley. Then, you will arrive at Tepecik village via a steep descent.
- A moderately challenging, enjoyable forest trail.

#### From Kırankurt valley to İnönü valley (12 km)

- You should set off on your way, taking water from the Kırankurt valley.
- The route starts on a dirt road. After 4 km, you lose about 500 metres of elevation over 1.5 km. You cross hillsides in places before taking the dirt road again to İnönü Highland.
- A moderately challenging, enjoyable forest trail.
- You can also start from the same route, beginning in the İnönü Valley.

#### From İnönü plateau to Sultaniye (25.5 km)

- There is a fountain on the road, but you should still take water from the İnönü plateau and set off.
- The trail, which is being followed on a forest road, will then transition into a steep descent after covering 12.5km.
- Medium difficulty, long course.
- You can travel starting from Sultaniye, using the same route, and reach the İnönü valley.

- There is a fountain on the road, but you should still take water from the İnönü plateau and set off.
- The trail, which is proceeding on a forest road, will initially involve a mix of uphill and downhill sections, before transitioning to a downhill route for the final 4km.
- A moderately difficult, long course. Its length can be challenging.
- You can reach İnönü Valley by starting from Serindere, using the same route.

#### From Aytepe to İnönü Valley and then to Serindere (35 km).

- There’s water available in the key areas. Therefore, you won’t experience water shortages.
- After passing the forest management building in Aytepe, the route begins at the fork leading to Menekşe Valley. First, you reach Ercuva Valley, and then İnönü Valley. There’s potable water in these areas. Afterwards, you continue on a dirt road towards Çilekli, and then reach Sultaniye, followed by a descent towards Serindere.
- A moderately difficult, long course. Its length can be challenging.
- You can reach İnönü Valley by starting from Serindere, using the same route.

#### From the secluded plateau of Saklı yaylası, to İnönü yaylası, and subsequently Ercuva yaylası (11 km).

- There are water points along the road.
- Initially, the route leads to Saklı yayla, then via a path through the forest, to İnönü yaylası. The route continues to Ercuva yaylası, and then returns to its starting point.
- A straightforward 11 km trail.
- You can set up camp in these three meadows. However, İnönü meadow is the most beautiful one.
