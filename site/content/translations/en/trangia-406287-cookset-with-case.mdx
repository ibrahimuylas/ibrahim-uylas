Trangia Cooking Set with Pot, Kettle, and Teflon Pan

This set, consisting of a saucepan, a Teflon frying pan, and a teapot, is particularly useful for hikers. With a total weight of 535 gr, this set is a preferred choice due to its weight and quality. It consists of a 2lt aluminum saucepan, a 18cm Teflon frying pan, and a 0.9lt aluminum teapot. This set, which fits inside each other, comes with a carrying case and a handle for placing and removing it from a stove.

If you’re looking for something well made, light, practical and compact, this is a good choice. It’s large for one person and easily serves 2, but it’s a bit of a stretch for 3. The non-stick frying pan in particular won’t be big enough for 3.

The same product is available with an aluminum pan instead of a Teflon pan. It would be beneficial to use a Teflon pan when camping, as cleaning food stuck to an aluminum pan can be quite difficult. You will find that this product, which you will surely love, will be an essential part of your camping kitchen and you will see that it is definitely worth the money.
