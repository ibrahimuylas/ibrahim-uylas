We liked Beğendik Cove, a Black Sea coast in Demirköy district of Kırklareli, close to the Bulgarian border. In this guide, you will find the travel option from İstanbul, my camping observations from June 2013, and the 50 km route along the Kıyıköy–İğneada–Beğendik line.

## Quick summary

- **Region:** Kırklareli, Demirköy–İğneada–Beğendik area
- Camp: During my visit in June 2013, the beach and the nearby wooded area were used.
- **Route:** Kıyıköy – İğneada – Beğendik, approximately 50 km
- Field observation: June 2013

> Update note: Camping permits, border zone regulations, businesses on the beach, road conditions and facilities may have changed since 2013. Before setting off, check for current local announcements, weather and sea conditions for Demirköy and Beğendik.

## Where is Beğendik Cove, and how do you get there?

In 2013, my journey began in İstanbul, where I traveled on the TEM highway towards Kırklareli, entering the Saray route. After Poyralı, I reached Beğendik via Demirköy and İğneada. ## Beğendik Bay campsite information

### Regarding my visit in June 2013

- There was a business/establishment located on the beach, and an area where a tent could be set up. I also found suitable flat areas on a wooded hill further down the coast.
- There was access to water and restrooms.
- We needed to go to Beğendik village for the market.
- The business located on the beach operated a cafeteria.
- The wide beach and the view of the Black Sea were the most captivating parts of the walk.

### What was missing on my June 2013 visit, and what to watch for

- I haven't seen any pensions, restaurants, or mosques.
- In the evening, there were large groups of people at the beach. The police/gendarmerie had warned us about potential problems; please check current local sources for security conditions.

Consider this list as a dated field record, not as current facility information.

- The route begins at the coast of Kıyıköy, following dirt roads within the Longoz Forests, and eventually leads to İğneada, and then to Beğendik Cove.
- Although the technical sections of the park run appear easy, its 50 km length requires significant endurance and a well-planned time schedule. You can choose to walk the entire route or just a portion of it.
- Carry enough water when you set off. Don’t count on the old water and supply points marked on the map still being in use.
- You can follow the route in the opposite direction from Beğendik Bay; be sure to check the access rules for the border area and forest beforehand.
