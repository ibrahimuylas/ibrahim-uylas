## Salomon SpeedCross 3 User Reviews

I've been using these shoes for over a year. Because there’s a significant difference compared to the GoreTex model, I chose to buy the regular version. I’m glad I didn’t spend the extra money on the Gore-Tex model. These shoes look really stylish and elegant, especially for mountain running. When you put them on, you say they’re incredibly comfortable, hugging your feet perfectly and supporting the sole. However, it turns out there are many drawbacks. First, I need to mention that the soles are smaller than those of other outdoor shoes. I wish I had bought half a size larger. And if you have “arak” (rock-shaped feet), you should definitely avoid these shoes. The front of the shoe is quite narrow, and even if your toes don’t touch the front, they’ll touch the top, which eventually causes pain in your nails. So, it’s not a comfortable shoe, and if you’re planning to use it for running, I recommend looking for something else. Another drawback is that the Salomon SpeedCross3 is very slippery, especially on wet and smooth surfaces. You could easily slip and injure yourself. Also, don’t buy these shoes for everyday wear in the city. Since these shoes are designed for rough terrain, the tread on the sole quickly wears down and becomes flat when used in the city. Ultimately, the design is beautiful, but because it doesn’t deliver the comfort and performance you expect, it’s been “taz”. I’ve heard similar complaints from other people who have used these shoes as well.
