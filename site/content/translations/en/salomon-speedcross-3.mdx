## Salomon SpeedCross 3 User Reviews

I've been using these shoes for over a year. Because there's a significant difference compared to the Gore-Tex model, I chose to buy the regular version. I'm glad I didn't spend the extra money on the Gore-Tex model. These shoes, designed specifically for mountain running, look quite stylish, elegant and beautiful. When you put them on, you think, "Oh, how comfortable, how well they hug your foot and support the entire sole." The sole is quite comfortable, and thanks to the lugs underneath, they grip the ground very well and deliver good performance. But here's the thing: there are many flaws. First, I must say that the lasts are smaller than those of other outdoor shoes. I thought, "I wish I'd bought half a size larger." Moreover, if you have wide feet like mine, stay away from these shoes. The front of the shoe is quite narrow. Even if your toes don't touch the front, they touch the upper part, which eventually causes pain in your nails. So, it's not a comfortable shoe, and if you plan to use it for running, I think you should look for another pair. Another flaw is that the Salomon SpeedCross3 is very slippery. It slips terribly on wet and flat ground. You could fall and break something. Also, don't buy these shoes to wear in the city. Designed for ground, the lugs on the sole wear down quickly and become flat when used in the city. In conclusion, the style is very nice, but because it doesn't deliver the comfort and performance expected of it, it's left me with a scratch. It's not just me; other acquaintances who have used these shoes have similar complaints.
