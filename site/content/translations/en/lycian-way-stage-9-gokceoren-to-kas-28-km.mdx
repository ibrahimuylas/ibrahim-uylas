## Gökçeören–Kaş: A long trail hike via Phellos

This page describes the 28 km ninth stage, from Gökçeören to Kaş, via Phellos and Çukurbağ. Its main characteristics are a cool, high starting point, a long trail section, the area around Phellos, and a steep descent to Kaş, recorded in 2014.

> **Historical field record:** This information is based on the experience of 28 September 2014. Water, facility, access, and camping conditions should not be considered current information.

### Stage summary

- **Stage:** 9 of 11
- **Start and finish:** Gökçeören – Kaş
- **Waypoints:** Phellos and Çukurbağ
- **Distance:** 28 km based on signposted distances
- **Walking time:** My journal says I covered the first 22 km in 5.5 hours. I set out around 09:00 and reached Kaş around 18:00. That interval includes stops.
- **Walked:** 28 September 2014
- **Current conditions:** No recent verification was available for this guide.

### Terrain, difficulty and navigation

According to the 2014 guide, the approximately 8 km dirt road following Gökçeören is mostly a trail. Signage was found to be sufficient on the trail; however, the writer, whose phone and GPS were not working, wrote in their log that they lost and found the signs four or five times. A steep descent was recorded near Kaş.

### Water and food — 2014 record

There is a noted lack of water sources between Gökçeören and Çukurbağ. Breakfast was served at the Gökçeören pension; no other specific food locations were recorded along the route. The current water and supply situation is unknown.

### Accommodation, camping and ways off the route — 2014 record

It is stated that there is a guesthouse and camping facility in Gökçeören, and that a flat camping area is visible immediately before reaching Kaş, descending steeply. The author spent the night at the home of a friend in Kaş. The internal exit option within Etap has not been determined.

### Notable places

Gökçeören Valley, Phellos Ancient City, Çukurbağ, and the approach from above to Kaş are the recorded points for this stage.

### What I learned on this stage

The ninth-day log reveals the impact of relying on a single navigation method for a long stretch. When the phone broke down, the efforts to find landmarks resulted in psychological exhaustion rather than physical fatigue.

- After Gökçeören, the route continues for approximately 8 km along a dirt road, then transitions to a trail.
- Gökçeören is located at an altitude above sea level, which can result in cooler temperatures.
- There’s a guesthouse in Gökçeören. It’s possible to stay here. A tent can be set up in the garden. Accommodation and shower needs can be met.
- When arriving in Kaş, there’s a steep descent from the top. This needs to be taken into consideration. Just before this descent, there’s a flat area where a tent can be set up.
