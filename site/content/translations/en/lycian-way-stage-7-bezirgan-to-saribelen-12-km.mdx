## Bezirgan–Sarıbelen: village crossing and a view of Kalkan

This page describes the seventh stage, which extends from Bezirgan to Sarıbelen, covering a distance of 12 km. Following the strenuous hike to Bezirgan, there’s a break in the village, followed by a stunning view of Kalkan–Patara as you approach the coast, and a short section with road access – all key details for the day.

> Historical field recording: The following practical information is based on the experience of 26 September 2014. Water, facility, transportation, and camping conditions should not be considered current information.

### Stage summary

- **Stage:** 7 of 11
- **Start and finish:** Bezirgan – Sarıbelen
- **Waypoints:** Bezirgan village and views of the Kalkan–Patara coastline
- **Distance:** 12 km based on signposted distances
- **Walking time:** The total walking time was not recorded.
- **Walked:** 26 September 2014
- **Current conditions:** No recent verification was available for this guide.

### Terrain, difficulty and navigation

In the 2014 guide, the section on Bezirgan is described as strenuous, while the subsequent part is presented as easier. Upon approaching Sarıbelen, the path led down to the sea, offering a scenic view, and the vehicle was taken off the road. The author clearly notes that during this vehicle section, they stopped for hitchhiking for approximately 2–3 km; this 12 km figure represents a historical stage.

### Water and food — 2014 record

It was noted that water could be obtained in the villages of Bezirgan and Sarıbelen, and that a village shop was located in Bezirgan. The diary mentions that the wells on the road to Bezirgan were empty, and that the water was taken from the fountain at the entrance to the village.

### Accommodation, camping and ways off the route — 2014 record

The author spent the night in a tent near Sarıbelen. Accommodation was booked at a pension in Bezirgan, but it wasn’t recorded; there was a pension in Sarıbelen, however, the price information was only reported. Current accommodation and departure options have not been determined.

### Notable places

Bezirgan village, with its views of the Kalkan and Patara coastlines, and the approach to Sarıbelen, were the most prominent sections of the route.

### What I learned on this stage

The seventh-day log highlights the importance of clearly stating the boundaries of a walking record: the author had discreetly noted that the route covered 2–3 km by hitchhiking. Therefore, the stage distance is not the continuous distance measured by GPS.
