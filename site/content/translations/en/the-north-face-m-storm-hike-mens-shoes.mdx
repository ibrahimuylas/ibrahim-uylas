It’s finally time to say something about these **outdoor shoes** that I’ve been using for about a year now. And I’m saying it now because, honestly, these shoes are still in great condition. You know, you wear a pair of shoes regularly for a year, and they just keep looking and feeling good. It’s amazing.

## How much, where did I get it, where can it be purchased

Before moving to England, I went to the rain country, saying that I need to learn to live with the rain. I bought a **Kamperest’ten North Face T932ZS**, saying “This is such a name, you know?” The price was the cheapest there, and I bought it around November 2017 for around 440 TL, and today, the price in December 2018 is around 539 TL. There hasn’t been a huge increase. I recommend researching online and buying from where you find the best price. Also, occasionally check outdoor stores – they sometimes offer remaining products at discounted prices.

## Why North Face T932ZS

I was looking for an **outdoor shoe** that would protect my feet from the rain and keep them warm, while being able to use it within the city. I really liked the style, and the price seemed more reasonable compared to other products. The fact that it was made with North Face and GoreTex also stood out, and what I liked most was how comfortable the sole was. 

## What happened in a year

I’ve been using these shoes regularly within the city for a year, and I continue to do so. Because I primarily wear them when going to the office, and I prefer my [**Lowa boots**](/lowa-zephyr-gtx-mid-bot-sage/) for nature walks, these shoes haven’t seen a lot of “nature.” I can certainly say that I walk a great deal within the city. Due to a year’s worth of use, the soles of the shoes have experienced a slight wear – but not significantly. Don’t imagine the soles completely worn out. I can confidently say that they are very suitable for city use. The photo was taken approximately two months ago, and their current condition is not significantly different from the photo. They still look very lively and comfortable. The only difference compared to their initial state is that the inner sole has lost some of the initial comfort, but they remain very comfortable nonetheless. These are shoes that you can use in spring, autumn, and summer, offering excellent value for the price and performance. 

That’s all I have to say. I tried to give you plenty of detail. They’re good shoes. If I wear them for 1 more year, that will take me to 24 months. Since I paid 440, that works out to about 18 TL a month, which is pretty good :)
