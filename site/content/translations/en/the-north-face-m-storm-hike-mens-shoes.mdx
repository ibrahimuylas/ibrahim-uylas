It’s finally time to say something about these **outdoor shoes** that I’ve been using for about a year now. And I’m saying it now because, honestly, these shoes are still in great condition. You know, you wear a pair of shoes regularly for a year, and they just keep looking and feeling good. It’s amazing.

## What did I pay, and where can you buy a pair?

Before moving to England, I went to the rain country, saying that I need to learn to live with the rain. I bought a **Kamperest’ten North Face T932ZS**, saying “This is such a name, you know?” The price was the cheapest there, and I bought it around November 2017 for around 440 TL, and today, the price in December 2018 is around 539 TL. There hasn’t been a huge increase. I recommend researching online and buying from where you find the best price. Also, occasionally check outdoor stores – they sometimes offer remaining products at discounted prices.

## Why North Face T932ZS

I was looking for an **outdoor shoe** that would protect my feet from the rain and keep them warm, while being suitable for use within the city. I liked the style, and the price seemed more reasonable compared to other products. The fact that it was North Face and Gore-Tex also stood out, and what I liked most was how comfortable the insole was. 

## What happened in a year

I’ve been using these shoes regularly within the city for a year, and I continue to do so. When I go to the office, I wear indoor shoes, and since I prefer my [**Lowa boots**](/lowa-zephyr-gtx-mid-bot-sage/) for nature walks, these shoes haven’t seen much of the outdoors. I can certainly say that I walk a great deal within the city. After a year of use, the lugs on the soles have worn down slightly – but not significantly. Don’t imagine the soles are completely worn out. I can confidently say that they are very suitable for city use. The photo above was taken about two months ago, and their current condition is no different from what’s in the photo. They still look very lively and comfortable. The only difference compared to their first days is that the insole has lost some of its original comfort, but they remain very comfortable nonetheless. These are shoes you can use in spring, autumn, and summer, offering excellent value for money and performance. 

That’s all I have to say. I tried to give you plenty of detail. They’re good shoes. If I wear them for 1 more year, that will take me to 24 months. Since I paid 440, that works out to about 18 TL a month, which is pretty good :)
