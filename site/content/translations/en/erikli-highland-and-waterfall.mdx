### Where is Erikli meadow and Erikli waterfall, and how do you get there?

Starting from Yalova, one first reaches Çınarcık. Following the Teşvikiye road, one then reaches Erikli Yaylası, and then Erikli Şelalesi. There’s a beautiful, paved road through the forest.

### Erikli meadow and Erikli waterfall camping area information

##### Available facilities

- You can set up a tent in Erikli yaylası.
- You can also set up a tent at Erikli Waterfall.
- There is a specialized business for the tent area.
- Parking
- Clean water
- Toilets
- Cafeteria(multiple)
- Prayer room
- Children’s Play Park
- Picnic area

##### Facilities that are unavailable

- Market (in Teşvikiye Köyü)

Note: This information dates back to 2014.

### Erikli Valley and Erikli Waterfall Hiking Routes

#### From Dipsiz Lake to Erikli Waterfall, and then on to the village of Teşvikiye (12.5 km) via the highland.

- The route begins at Dipsiz Lake and proceeds through the forest, eventually reaching Erikli Waterfall. Afterwards, the route continues towards Erikli Valley, extending through the forest up to the entrance of Teşvikiye village.
- There are water points along the road.
- Medium difficulty course.
- You can also do it starting from Teşvikiye.

#### From the Delmece valley, to the Erikli waterfall, and then on to the Teşvikiye village (12.4 km).

- The route begins in Delmece valley and, after proceeding a short distance along a dirt road, reaches Erikli waterfall. Then, the route continues through the forest, extending as far as the entrance to Teşvikiye village.
- There are water points along the road.
- Medium difficulty course.
- You can also do it starting from Teşvikiye.

#### From Kocadere, followed by Dipsiz göl, then Erikli waterfall and the surrounding valley, and finally to the village of Teşvikiye (16.5 km).

- The route begins in Kocadere, ascending through a dirt path within the forest. After approximately 550 meters of elevation gain, you will reach Dipsiz Lake. From Dipsiz Lake, the route descends again, continuing through the forest to reach Erikli Waterfall. The route then extends through the forest, leading to the entrance of Teşvikiye village.
- There are water points along the road.
- Medium difficulty course.
- You can also do it starting from Teşvikiye.

#### Following the trip to Şenköy and then to Dipsiz göl, we continued to Erikli şelalesi and the surrounding valley, and finally to Teşvikiye köyüne (20 km).

- The route begins in Şenköy and ascends through a dirt path within the forest. After approximately 550 meters of elevation gain, you will reach Dipsiz Lake. Following Dipsiz Lake, the route descends through the forest, leading you to Erikli Waterfall. Continuing onward, the route extends through the forest, reaching the entrance to Teşvikiye village.
- There are water points along the road.
- Medium difficulty course.
- You can also do it starting from Teşvikiye.

#### From Erikli waterfall to Delmece valley (9.5 km)

- The trail begins at the Erikli waterfall, following the stream it flows into, and leads through the forest to Delmece Valley.
- Take the water from Erikli waterfall before leaving.
- Medium difficulty course.
- You can also do the same route starting at Delmece Highland. It will be easier in this direction because you’ll be going downhill.

#### From Selimiye village, in the Delmece valley, and then on to Erikli Waterfall (18 km)

- Turn left into the forest at the end of Selimiye village to pick up the trail. It reaches Delmece Highland after about 7 km. Follow the dirt road past Dipsiz Lake and on to Erikli Waterfall.
- Do not take your water before leaving Selimiye village. Afterwards, there is drinking water available in Delmece valley and at the Erikli waterfall.
- Medium difficulty course.
- You can also do it using the same route, starting from the Erikli Waterfall.
