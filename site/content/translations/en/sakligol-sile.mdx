Saklıgöl is a recreation area. It’s a lovely lake with a business where most visitors come for breakfast. You can dream of pitching a tent near the lake, but that’s where the dream ends. The lakeside doesn’t suit those of us who want to camp somewhere quiet, alone with nature. You could look for a good spot in the woods beyond the lake. I haven’t pitched at Saklıgöl in Şile, so I can’t tell you exactly where. There are lots of trails in the area for day walks.

### Where is Şile Saklıgöl, and how do you get there?

This small pond near İstanbul is about 60 km from Ataşehir. Take the Şile road out of İstanbul. At the turn for Şile you’ll see the signs for Saklıgöl on the right. Follow that road to Saklıgöl.

### Şile Saklıgöl camping area information

##### Available facilities

- You won’t be able to pitch a tent at Saklıgöl with peace of mind. The crowds and its status as a recreational area will get in the way of the tranquility you’re looking for. So, if you venture a bit into the forest tracks around Saklıgöl, you can pitch a tent in the flat areas you’ll find.
- Because I haven’t set up a tent here, I can’t pinpoint the exact spot. However, as I said, after crossing the lake, there are places where people have set up tents within the forest – you can do the same.
- Even if you were to utilize the facility in Saklıgöl, you can obtain all your camping needs from Şile.
- Despite the existence of camping areas in the Şile area, there isn’t a private business offering camping here.
- There’s a restaurant serving breakfast and other meals.
- You may use this business’s restroom.
- It’s a place that’s too crowded to visit on the weekend. If you can, I recommend going during the week.

##### Facilities that are unavailable

- There aren't any hotels, guesthouses, or similar places.
- There isn’t really a business like that, or anything like a market.

Note: This information dates back to February 2017.

- The route begins in Şile Saklıgöl and extends through forest paths to reach the Darlık Dam.
- As you approach sunset, you can enjoy beautiful views near Darlık Dam.
- We took two cars and left one at the Darlık dam. That way, we could also pick up the car we'd left at the starting point.
- A relatively easy and approximately 4-hour trail.
- You can refill your water from the fountain on the park path.

- The route begins in Şile Saklıgöl and continues through forest paths, eventually returning to Şile Saklıgöl in a circular manner.
- Walking in the spring months would be more enjoyable than walking in the summer heat.
- It’s an easy route. You can park your car at the business’s parking area by the lake.
- Don’t forget to take your water with you.
