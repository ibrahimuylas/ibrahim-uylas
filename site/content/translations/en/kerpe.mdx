### Where is Kerpe and how do you get there?

Starting from İzmit, by taking the route to Kandıra, you can travel towards the Black Sea and first reach Kandıra. Then, you can reach Kerpe by following the signs for Babaköy.

You can set up a camp at the location shown on the Google Maps, and have a picnic there. (We paid a vehicle fee of 30 TL, and they were charging 15 TL for the picnic area.)

- As of 2017, I will provide the current information.

  - They charge 50 TL per vehicle for camping, whether you pitch 1 tent or 4. The picnic fee is 20 TL.
  - 50 TL for a tent is a lot of money. And there’s no proper service in return. They’re just allocating space.
  - Despite being at a sea-facing location, the beach is incredibly dirty. People apparently swim here in the summer. I’m amazed to think anyone would swim on this beach in the summer.
  - The area behind the marked point is wooded. You can find a suitable spot within this wooded area and set up your tent there.

##### Available facilities

- There are also private campsites available within Kerpe.
- Drinking Water
- Toilets
- Market (available in Kerpe Merkez)
- Restaurant (located in Kerpe Merkez)
- Cafeteria (located in Kerpe Merkez)
- Coast

### Kerpe Hiking Trails

#### From Kandıra to Kerpe (20 km)

  frameBorder='0'
  scrolling='no'

  style={{ borderRadius: '10px 10px' }}

- The route begins in Kandıra, proceeding inland on a dirt road, and eventually reaching the coast of the Black Sea. Continuing along the parallel coastline, you arrive in Kerpe.
- There are places along the route with drinking water, but still, set off from Kandıra with your water bottles fully topped up.
- It’s an easy course, but its length may be challenging for you.

#### From Kefken to Kerpe (4 km)

<iframe
  frameBorder='0'
  scrolling='no'
  src='https://tr.wikiloc.com/wikiloc/spatialArtifacts.do?event=view&id=3050190&measures=on&title=on&near=off&images=on&maptype=H'
  width='100%'
  height='600'
  style={{ borderRadius: '10px 10px' }}
></iframe>

- Start somewhere between Kerpe and Kefken and follow the dirt track through the forest to the Black Sea coast. Continue along the shore, sometimes in the water and sometimes over the rocks, until you reach Kerpe.
- Take water with you before you start the trail.
- Medium difficulty course.

#### From Kerpe, then to Soğukpınar, followed by Sarısu (8 km)

<iframe
  frameBorder='0'
  scrolling='no'
  src='https://tr.wikiloc.com/wikiloc/spatialArtifacts.do?event=view&id=5456550&measures=on&title=on&near=off&images=on&maptype=H'
  width='100%'

- To begin the route, you first need to get to the Soğukpınar trout farm near Kerpe. After passing the trout farm, you'll continue through the forest. You'll need to cross the stream on both sides. After 1.5km, you'll continue on a forest dirt road, passing around Babaköy and reaching Sarısu.
- Take water with you before you start the trail.
- You might encounter a wild boar.
- The initial 1.5km stretch can be a bit tiring. It’s a moderately challenging course.

#### From Kerpe, to Kurtyeri village, and then via Kefken back to Kerpe (14.5 km).

- You will begin the route in Kerpe, following an unpaved forest path to the village of Kurtyeri. Then, you will continue along an unpaved forest path to Kefke, and from there, return to Kerpe, following the coastline.
- Take water with you before you start the trail.
- It’s an easy course.
