In August 2014, I walked a 57 km route with my brother Furkan, starting in Yalova and ending in Fıstıklı. Our route followed Yalova, Termal, Soğuksu Şelalesi, Selimiye Köyü, and Fıstıklı. This is not a current route guide, but rather a short summary and video recording of a long walk we took in hot weather.

## Route summary

* **Beginning:** Yalova
* Fıstıklı
* Transition points: Thermal, Soğuksu Waterfall and Selimiye Village
* Total distance: 57 km
* Hiking date: August 2014

After taking that break during Ramadan, we were absolutely exhausted. We decided to go out and walk, ignoring the heat. Because there would be heavy Bayram traffic, we needed a route that wouldn't involve driving; therefore, we chose Yalova. It was a very hot journey. There won’t be a detailed explanation here, the whole story is in the video. Enjoy!

## When planning this route

57 km is not a short nature walk to be undertaken unprepared. Divide the route according to your fitness level; before setting off, double-check the weather conditions, current road conditions, water sources, and return transport. This record may not represent current changes to the route, as it is based on our experience in 2014.
