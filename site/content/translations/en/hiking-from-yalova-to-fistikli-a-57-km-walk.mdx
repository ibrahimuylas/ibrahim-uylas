In August 2014, I walked a 57 km route with my brother Furkan, starting in Yalova and ending in Fıstıklı. Our route followed Yalova, Termal, Soğuksu Şelalesi, Selimiye Köyü, and Fıstıklı. This is not a current route guide, but rather a short summary and video recording of a long walk we took in hot weather.

## Route summary

* **Beginning:** Yalova
* Fıstıklı
* Transition points: Thermal, Soğuksu Waterfall and Selimiye Village
* Total distance: 57 km
* Hiking date: August 2014

After our break for Ramadan we were absolutely itching to get out again. Who cared about the heat? With the holiday traffic, we wanted a route we could reach without driving, so we chose Yalova. We won’t forget that heat in a hurry. No blow-by-blow account this time. The whole story’s in the video. Enjoy!

## When planning this route

57 km is not a short nature walk to be undertaken unprepared. Divide the route into stages according to your fitness level; before setting off, double-check the weather, current trail conditions, water points, and return transport. This account describes our 2014 experience, so it may not reflect current changes to the route.
