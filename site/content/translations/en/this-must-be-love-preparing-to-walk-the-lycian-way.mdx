This is a personal account detailing the preparation I undertook over several months before the 2014 Lycian Way hike, focusing on the “Lycian passion” – the excitement and extensive preparation for the journey.

## My note from before the 2014 hike

9 September 2014

I first heard his name about a year ago. By February 2014, I’d decided to prepare for our meeting. September would be a good date, weather-wise. To be ready by 20 September, I started exercising. To be ready for the meeting, I added many friends to my routine, training regularly four days a week, starting eight months in advance. In the last two months, I stopped playing hall football matches to avoid injury. Even though I could have said a lot about quitting the matches, I had to be ready for the meeting day. As the days passed, I was both dreaming about the meeting and starting my research. Although it seemed like there was more time, it was such a huge passion that it couldn’t be squeezed into a short period. In recent days, I realized I was talking about him like a madman to everyone. I told every person I spoke to about him, described him, and talked about my interest in him. Until late at night, I was either exercising or continuing my research. Every time I closed my eyes, I felt like I was with him. When I reached the last two weeks, I had started seeing him even without closing my eyes. I couldn’t focus on anything. I’d suddenly drift into thoughts about him, then snap back to reality, wondering where I was. While trying to sort out my diet and sleep routine with just two weeks left until the meeting, I realized it was getting worse. My appetite was gone, and I was now sleeping only 4–5 hours a day. I didn’t want to think about what came next. I didn’t want the future to happen. Excitement had taken over every part of me.

Only love could make you do all this. Love for the Lycian Way…
