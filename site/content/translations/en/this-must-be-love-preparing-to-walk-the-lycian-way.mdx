## My note from before the 2014 hike

9 September 2014

I first heard his name about a year ago. I decided to arrange a meeting in February 2014. September was a good date, considering the circumstances. I started exercising in order to be ready by September 20th. To be ready for the meeting, I added many friends, starting with 8 months in advance, and I continued exercising regularly four days a week. Although I stopped playing hall court matches in the last two months to avoid injury, I was determined to be ready for the meeting. While the days passed like this, I was both dreaming about the meeting and starting my research. Although it seemed like there was more time, it was a very big passion that couldn't be squeezed into a short time. Lately, I realized that I was telling everyone about him constantly, describing him, and talking about my interest in him. I was exercising until late at night or continuing my research. I felt like he was with me every time I closed my eyes. When I got to the last two weeks, I started seeing him without even closing my eyes. I couldn't focus on anything. I was suddenly lost in thoughts about him. Then, I was surprised by where I was. While trying to establish a healthy diet and sleep routine with only two weeks left until the meeting, I realized it was getting worse. My appetite was gone, and I was now only sleeping 4-5 hours a day. I didn’t want to think about anything further. I didn’t want to think about the future. The excitement had taken over me completely.

All of this could only be made to feel by a human – Lycian Love…
