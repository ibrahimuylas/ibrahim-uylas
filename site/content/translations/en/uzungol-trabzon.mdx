title: "Uzungöl, Trabzon"
description: "A lot had changed at Uzungöl in the ten years since my last visit. Then I met someone who remembered the place fifty years back."
---

Ten years ago, I came to this beautiful natural place, and so much has changed. Back then, there was hardly anything apart from 1-2 guesthouses, 1-2 cafeterias, and 1 market. When I said this, an uncle replied, “I know this place from 50 years ago, what are you talking about? Just look at it now.” These days, everywhere is packed with guesthouses, hotels, and various businesses. There’s no empty space left. Even if there were, you couldn’t pitch a tent—it’s banned. In fact, they’ve even put up a sign saying bicycles aren’t allowed on this road by the lake. I won’t drag this on, but when I saw it, I was really upset. I thought they’d completely swallowed Uzungöl. Despite all this, the lake’s beauty remains. For those who want to go, here are the transport details:

### Trabzon Uzungöl: Where is it and how do you get there?

First, you reach Of via the Black Sea coast road. Then, by following the Çaykara road, you go to Uzungöl. You reach Uzungöl by approximately 45km on asphalt road, starting from the coastal road. The route is one-lane, winding, and takes approximately one hour.

### Trabzon Uzungöl camping area information

##### Available facilities

- There are one or two privately run campsites. With so many day visitors, it’s hard to find a comfortable spot to pitch here. If you can, head towards Demirkapı village and its highland pasture, or try Galester Highland.
- Restaurant
- Café
- Merchandise sellers, including those selling gifts.
- Grocery shop
- Toilets
- Amusement Park
- Children’s Play Park
- Parking
- Hotel, guesthouse
- Mosque

Note: This information dates back to September 2015.
