Ten years ago, things have changed a lot in this beautiful natural place where I came. Ten years ago, there were only 1-2 guesthouses, 1-2 cafeterias, and 1 market. When I say this, an uncle said, “I remember this place from 50 years ago, what do you say?” And then he said, “Just look at it now.” Now, it’s completely full of guesthouses, hotels, and various businesses. There’s no empty space. Even if there were, you couldn’t set up a tent – it’s prohibited. In fact, they’ve even put up a sign saying that you can’t drive a bicycle on this road by the lake. I won’t go on much longer, but when I saw it, I was very saddened. I thought they had completely swallowed Uzungöl. Despite all this, the beauty of the lake is being preserved. For those who want to go, here are the transportation details:

### Trabzon Uzungöl: Where is it and how do you get there?

First, you reach Of via the Black Sea coast road. Then, by following the Çaykara road, you go to Uzungöl. You reach Uzungöl by approximately 45km on asphalt road, starting from the coastal road. The route is one-lane, winding, and takes approximately one hour.

### Trabzon Uzungöl camping area information

##### Available facilities

- You can find a limited number of private camping sites – approximately 1-2. There’s no suitable area for setting up a tent. This spot is very popular with day-trippers, and you won’t be able to relax here. If possible, you could go towards Demirkapı village and the surrounding area, or towards Galester plateau.
- Restaurant
- Café
- Merchandise sellers, including those selling gifts.
- Grocery shop
- Toilets
- Amusement Park
- Children’s Play Park
- Parking
- Hotel, guesthouse
- Mosque

Note: This information dates back to September 2015.
