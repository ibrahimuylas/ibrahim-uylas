## Körmen Island – Kaleüçağız: Final via Boğazcık and Aperlai

This page describes the eleventh stage, which extends 23 km from opposite Körmen Island via Boğazcık and Aperlai to Kaleüçağız. The paths rising from the coast, scarce water points, and consecutive directional losses defined the final day of the 2014 hike.

> Historical field record: The following practical information is based on a 30 September 2014 experience. Water, facility, access, and camping conditions should not be considered current information.

### Stage summary

- **Stage:** 11 of 11
- **Start and finish:** Körmen Island to Kaleüçağız
- **Waypoints:** Boğazcık, Aperlai and the coast at Üçağız
- **Distance:** 23 km based on signposted distances
- **Walking time:** The total walking time was not recorded.
- **Walked:** 30 September 2014
- **Current conditions:** Current conditions have not been checked, and no source for verifying them was identified when this guide was prepared.

### Terrain, difficulty and navigation

In 2014, the route initially progressed along the coast, then turned left and climbed up to Boğazcık; after Aperlai, it veered off the coast and turned back towards the sea. The author wrote that they lost the route three times that day, fell on the rocks at the last deviation and hit their arm, and subsequently finished the hike in Kaleüçağız.

### Water and food — 2014 record

The 2014 notes list no water before Boğazcık. There was water in the village, food at its guesthouses and a place to eat in Aperlai. Back on the coast beyond Aperlai, cold water came out between the rocks. The present condition of these sources and businesses is unknown.

### Accommodation, camping and ways off the route — 2014 record

I recorded guesthouses in Boğazcık, a venue at Aperlai and guesthouses and cafés in Kaleüçağız. I spent the night in a tent next to a sarcophagus in Kaleüçağız. That account does not establish current permission to camp. No regular exit option was identified along this stage.

### Notable places

The main stops on the route include the coast of Körmen Island, Boğazcık, the ancient city of Aperlai, and the Kaleüçağız section.

### What I learned on this stage

The first day’s log reveals that accumulated physical and psychological fatigue can exacerbate errors in navigation. The author, after falling on a rocky area, decided not to prolong the risk and ended their 11-day hike in Kaleüçağız.

- While continuing from this spot across from Körmen island, you may find the course confusing.
- First, you proceed from the sea shore, and then you go left, climbing upwards to reach the village of Boğazcık.
- There’s no water until Boğazcık. You can refill there and may find something to eat at the guesthouses.
- There is no water to Aperlai. There is one facility in Aperlai. You can take a break here and eat.
- While traveling from Aperlai’den to Kaleüçağız, the path will again turn to the left and go uphill. You should carefully follow the signs.
- While traveling from Aperlai’den to Kaleüçağız, the route initially deviates from the coastline and then returns to it. You reach Kaleüçağız by following the coastline. There’s a place here where cold water flows through the rocks. It will be on your path; just pay attention, and you’ll see it.
- I pitched my tent beside an ancient sarcophagus in Kaleüçağız and spent the night there.
- In Kaleüçağız, there are countless guesthouses and cafes. However, they are a bit expensive. Just so you know.
