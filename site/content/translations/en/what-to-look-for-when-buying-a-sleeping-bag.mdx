## What does a sleeping bag do?

The primary purpose of sleeping bags isn’t to heat you up. Their main function is to maintain your body temperature while you sleep. You warm the environment, and the sleeping bag provides insulation against the outside, so you can sleep comfortably without getting cold.

## What features matter in a sleeping bag?

There are several important things to keep in mind when buying a sleeping bag.

### Sleeping bag temperature ratings

sleeping bags featuring a brand have upper limit, comfort, lower limit, and extreme temperature values. The upper limit temperature is the value at which you can sleep without excessive sweating. The comfort temperature is the value at which you can sleep comfortably. The lower limit is the temperature at which you may occasionally feel slightly cold while sleeping. The extreme temperature is the lower limit for survival in an emergency. Most branded sleeping bags will have these three values: upper limit, comfort, and extreme temperature. The values in a branded sleeping bag may differ according to gender. You won't find such detailed information in unbranded or simpler sleeping bags.

### Types of insulation

Two different sleeping bags are being produced, made of both synthetic and goose down. Goose down sleeping bags are more durable than synthetic sleeping bags – they take up less space and are lighter. Goose down sleeping bags contain a mixture of goose down/feather. A sleeping bag with a high percentage of goose down is considered more effective. Because we know how goose down is obtained, you may not want to purchase it. Therefore, you should opt for a synthetic sleeping bag. It is said that synthetic sleeping bags dry faster than those with goose down, when wet. This is presented as a significant disadvantage for goose down sleeping bags. Do not believe this. A good camping sleeping bag should not get wet. Obtaining goose down presents a valid reason not to choose goose down. Some sleeping bags have outer fabrics that are more resistant to moisture/wetting. Be sure to consider this when choosing a sleeping bag.

### Types of Sleeping Bags

There are sleeping bags available in mummy and blanket shapes. The blanket-shaped ones are suitable for summer. However, I recommend getting the mummy-style sleeping bag. It offers more comfort and insulation by only allowing the mouth area to remain open. And, being able to tie it around your head and neck allows you to further reduce heat loss.

### Where and when will you use it?

Where and in which seasons you’ll use the sleeping bag are the biggest factors when choosing one. If you won’t be camping in winter on a highland at 2,000 metres, you don’t need a comfort rating of -16°C. Find out the lowest overnight temperatures for the places and season in which you plan to camp, and choose accordingly. Town centres and higher ground have different temperatures. Looking at the forecast for Kocaeli can mislead you if you’re camping on a highland in Kocaeli. Remember that the temperature drops by about 1°C for each 150 metres of elevation gained. If you live in İstanbul and only camp nearby in places like the highlands or the coast outside winter, a sleeping bag with a comfort rating of 0°C will be more than enough.
