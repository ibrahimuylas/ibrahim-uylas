Hiking is a walk in nature that you usually finish the same day, carrying a light backpack. Distance alone does not tell you how demanding a route will be. Consider how long it takes, how difficult it is and what preparation it requires.

## At a glance

- **Duration:** Usually a day trip
- **Route:** A marked trail, forest road or mountain path
- **Pack:** Usually 15–30 litres
- **Essentials:** Water, food, layers, navigation and emergency supplies
- **How it differs from trekking:** Hiking usually requires neither overnight accommodation nor packing for multiple days

## What does hiking mean?

Unlike a walk through town, a hike is a planned walk on natural terrain. A short woodland trail and a long mountain route with plenty of elevation gain can both be hikes. Look at elevation, terrain, weather and daylight alongside the distance.

On group walks, people moving at different speeds can split into smaller groups. Agree on meeting points, the pace of the slowest walker and a return time before you start to reduce the risk of getting separated.

## What is the difference between hiking and trekking?

A hike usually finishes the same day, either where you started or at a planned endpoint. A trek typically involves several days, stages and overnight stops. Usage varies between countries. For both, prepare for the conditions you expect to encounter.

## What should you pack for a day hike?

- Enough water for the route and snacks for energy
- A rain or wind layer
- Sun protection, a hat and a spare layer suitable for the season
- A phone, an offline map and a power bank
- A small first-aid kit, a whistle and a head torch
- A rubbish bag and any personal medication

See the [hiking gear checklist](/doga-yuruyusleri-icin-gerekli-ekipmanlar/) for a more detailed list by season.

## How should you choose your first hiking route?

For your first hike, pick a well-marked trail with mobile reception along parts of the route and somewhere you can leave it quickly if needed. Check the total ascent and the terrain before judging the route by distance. Weather can change quickly, so decide on your route, approximate return time and emergency contact in advance.
