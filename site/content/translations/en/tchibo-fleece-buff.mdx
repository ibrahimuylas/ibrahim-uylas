Tchibo Polar Buff Product Review

The thing that’s really on my mind in this photo is precisely this model, which is produced as a double-layer fleece neck warmer. It’s a lifesaver in winter and in windy weather – you can use it both during nature walks and in everyday life. Thanks to the fleece buff, my neck and face are protected from wind and freezing cold. This double-layer neck warmer has one side made of soft Single Jersey and the other side is fleece. Its functional material provides optimum heat insulation, allows air circulation, and is extremely lightweight. Thanks to the special Antipilling material, it doesn’t pill.

This product offers excellent value at an affordable price.
