---
title: "Embarrassed, Surprised and Happy"
description: "Date: 17 October 2013."
---
Date: 17 October 2013. It was a beautiful autumn time, and I had set off on the road to Yedigöller. With my 25kg pack, I had climbed 14km uphill, well outside my usual limits. The sky was darkening, and I had arrived at the small alpine meadow where I planned to camp, which I had found via Google Maps. There were about 8–10 alpine huts in the meadow. While I was expecting to find a number of people in the meadow, what I found was that there was absolutely no one there. Well, no problem – my aim was to pitch my tent, eat something simple, and then lie down to sleep. The sky was darkening, and I was in the process of pitching my tent when a car arrived. A family living in the meadow had gone out for a day trip, and they had stopped to say hello. They initially gave me a bit of a "look," and then they found me a house with a stove and began to insist that I move in. I said, "Why would I stay in someone else's house when I have my own tent? I'll be warm and comfortable, sleeping like that." They replied, "Here, our alpine huts are left unlocked – we leave them open so that travellers and hunters can come and stay." Then, to avoid being caught in the dark, they left before the sun set. I lit the stove, made a meal, and also prepared some tea. Oh, what more could I ask for? I could easily spend a week here. I had just finished a 14km climb uphill, and I was so out of breath that my mouth and nose were almost touching; I had pushed myself to the limit, so a warm place with a stove had been just what I needed. It was a very nice night. Then I went to Yedigöller. On the way back, I stayed one more night in this house, and together with the extra food I had, I left, having written a note of thanks. It had turned out to be a strenuous trekking tour lasting five days. Months later, one day, I was contacted by an unknown number. The person calling was İlhami abi, the owner of the house in the meadow. He introduced himself first, and then continued as follows: "We came and cleaned up the place; the snow had cleared. It's a very beautiful place – you can come and stay again." I was overwhelmingly embarrassed, surprised, and happy, and I conveyed my thanks.

Right now, when I’m explaining these things again, I feel embarrassed, confused, and happy :)
