---
title: "Embarrassed, Surprised and Happy"
description: "On the way to Yedigöller in October 2013 I spent the night in someone else’s mountain hut. Months later, the owner rang me."
---
Date: 17 October 2013. It was a beautiful autumn time, and I had set off for Yedigöller. With my 25kg pack, I had climbed 14km uphill, my tongue hanging out. The sky was darkening, and I had arrived at the small yayla where I planned to camp, which I had found via Google Maps. There were about 8–10 yayla houses. Expecting to find a crowd of people in the highland settlement, what did I see? No one at all. Well, never mind; my aim was to pitch my tent, grab something to eat on the go, and sleep immediately. The sky was darkening, and I was trying to pitch my tent when a car arrived. A family who had houses in the highland settlement had gone out for a day trip and decided to drop by. First, they gave me a bit of a scolding: "Why don't you go into one of the houses and stay?" Then they found me a house with a stove and started forcing me to move in. I kept saying, "Why do I need to stay in someone else's house? I have my own tent; I won't be cold, I'll sleep comfortably," and so on. They replied, "Our yayla houses here are not locked. They are left open so that travellers or hunters can come and stay." Then, to avoid being caught in the dark, they left before the sun set. I lit the stove, made a meal, and brewed some tea. Oh, what more could I ask for? I could spend a week here. I had just climbed that 14km uphill, my mouth and nose were all mixed up, I was completely exhausted, so a warm place with a stove was exactly what I needed. I had a very nice night. Then I went to Yedigöller. On the way back, I stayed one more night in this house, and I left with the extra food I had, along with a note of thanks. It had been a strenuous five-day trekking tour. Months later, one day, I was called by an unknown number. The caller was İlhami abi, the owner of the house in the highland settlement. He introduced himself first, and then continued: "We came and cleaned up the place; the snow has melted. It's a very beautiful place again, you can come and stay." I was overwhelmingly embarrassed, surprised, and happy, and I conveyed my thanks.

Right now, when I’m explaining these things again, I feel embarrassed, confused, and happy :)
