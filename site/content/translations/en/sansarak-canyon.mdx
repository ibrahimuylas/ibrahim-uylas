### Where is Sansarak Canyon and how do you get there?

From Yalova, go via Orhangazi towards İznik. Before entering İznik, take the left turn for Sansarak village. The road is about 18.5 km and mostly paved. About 1.5 km beyond the village, look for the entrance sign on the left that says “Kanyon 1 Şelale”.

![İznik, Sansarak Canyon, Waterfall Entrance](../../posts/rotalar/sansarak-kanyonu/iznik-sansarak-kanyonu-gıiris-1.jpg 'İznik, Sansarak Canyon, Waterfall Entrance')

#### Canyon 1 Waterfall Entrance

Here, you follow the forest trail down to the canyon. After descending into the canyon, you will see the waterfall by turning to the left. Continuing to the right, and then walking upstream along the stream, you will head towards the “Değirmen” entrance, which is the second entrance. If you started at “Kanyon 1” waterfall entrance and want to reach “Kanyon 2”’s “Değirmen” entrance, you will take a long walk, and follow the dirt path on your right.

![İznik, Sansarak Canyon, Değirmen Entrance](../../posts/rotalar/sansarak-kanyonu/iznik-sansarak-kanyonu-gıiris-2.jpg 'İznik, Sansarak Canyon, Değirmen Entrance')

#### Canyon 2 Mill Entrance

You will see the other sign approximately 3.5 km after leaving the village, on the left. Following the path descending to the left from this sign, you will reach the stream. Following the stream, you will proceed through the canyon. As you continue to descend to the left, you will be heading towards the entrance of Canyon 1 Waterfall. If you wish to exit from the Canyon 1 Waterfall path, there’s a point where you might find it difficult to locate a sign within the trail. While descending into the canyon, after approximately 2-3 hours, the stream will suddenly widen, and you will feel like you’ve reached the end of the canyon. After walking for another 5 minutes from this point, you will reach the large rocks where the waterfall is located. When you arrive at the wide area, you will see a slope to your left, with a forest. Looking into it a bit, you will be able to see the trail. Following this trail will lead you to the entrance of Canyon 1 Waterfall. It would be nice if there were a sign at this point. If I were to go back, I would put a sign here.

### Sansarak Canyon camping area information

##### Available facilities

- Camp Site (There isn't a private business, but there are flat areas where tents can be set up).
- Clean water
- Grocery store (located within the village)

##### Facilities that are unavailable

- WC (Although a bit distant, you can use the mosque toilets inside the village.)
- Restaurant
- Guesthouse

Note: This information dates back to 2013.

### Sansarak Canyon Hiking Trails

#### From the Sansarak village, then to the Sansarak canyon, and then back to Sansarak village (10 km).

- There are water points along the route. You can obtain your water from the Sansarak village. In addition, instead of entering the canyon, you should fill your water from the fountain on the road, and then enter the canyon. There are no water points inside the canyon.
- Starting in Sansarak village, this route begins with a walk on a dirt road, leading you to the entrance of Canyon 1 Waterfall. Then, you continue through a forest trail to reach the canyon. Following this, you proceed along the canyon, observing it as it ascends, and eventually reach the entrance of Canyon 2 – the Mill. After exiting from Canyon 2 – the Mill entrance, you return to the village again, using a dirt road.
- While walking on the dirt road, you will be exposed to the dust from vehicles passing by. Despite this, the view is quite beautiful, and when you reach the canyon via the forest trail, you’ll be glad you came.
- You can complete the route by entering from the Kanyon 2 – Değirmen entrance and descending through the canyon, exiting from the Kanyon 1 – Şelale entrance. Descending into the canyon will be easier.
- Watch out for slippery rocks and stones covered in moss in the canyon.

- There are water points along the route. You can obtain your water from the Sansarak village. In addition, instead of entering the canyon, you should fill your water from the fountain on the road, and then enter the canyon. There are no water points inside the canyon.
- Start at the “Kanyon 1 Şelale” (Waterfall) entrance and take the path through the forest to the canyon. Follow the canyon upstream towards the “Kanyon 2 Değirmen” (Mill) entrance. After you exit at Kanyon 2 Değirmen, you’ll reach a dirt road.
- Once you reach the trail within the forest and the canyon, you’ll be glad you came.
- You can complete the route by entering from the Kanyon 2 – Değirmen entrance and descending through the canyon, exiting from the Kanyon 1 – Şelale entrance. Descending into the canyon will be easier.
- Watch out for slippery rocks and stones covered in moss in the canyon.

- You can obtain water from the Elmalı village. You may experience a water problem further down the road.
- This route, starting in Elmalı village, involves a 400-meter ascent over 2 kilometers, so you’ll get a bit tired. Afterwards, the route continues with a descent, leading you into the canyon from the Değirmen entrance. You will then cross the waterfall and reach Sansarak Village via the dirt road on the mountainside.
- At the point where the walk ended, there is a fountain.
- You will have gone through the entire canyon.
- Watch out for slippery rocks and stones covered in moss in the canyon.

- On this route, which is approximately 11 km long and rises by about 800 meters, you will see very beautiful views.
- Medium difficulty course.
- Sometimes you will proceed through the forest, and sometimes also from a dirt road.

- You should start by filling your water supply from the İhsaniye village, and set off. You may encounter a water problem along the route.
- A challenging route consisting of a mountain path, a tractor path, and occasionally a path through the forest, with ups and downs.
- It’s a course that’s somewhere between medium and difficult. The descents and ascents can be quite tiring.
- It is advised for anyone wishing to enter the forest.
