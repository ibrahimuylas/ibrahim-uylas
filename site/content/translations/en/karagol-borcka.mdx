One of Artvin’s most beautiful and natural lakes, and definitely worth a visit.

### Where is Borçka Karagöl, and how do you get there?

After reaching Borçka via either Hopa or Artvin, follow the road from Borçka centre to Karagöl. After completing the approximately 21 km of asphalt, follow the Karagöl sign and turn onto the dirt track. You’ll reach Karagöl after about 7 km on this lovely forest road, where only two vehicles can barely squeeze past, some sections are in poor condition, and the trees occasionally block your view of the sky.

### Şavşat Karagöl camping area information

##### Available facilities

- Camp Site (8 TL – per tent)
- Clean water
- Toilets
- Parking (7 TL)

##### Facilities that are unavailable

- Grocery shop
- Restaurant
- The guesthouse (it was only located on the Karagöl road.)

Note: This information dates back to 2011.
