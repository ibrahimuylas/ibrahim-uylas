For two days, we had been in Pokut plateau, and we were about to separate when we encountered someone. The next day, they mentioned going to Verçenik plateau in Çat valley. “Verçenik plateau sounds really nice,” they added. “After Pokut, it’s the plateau I’ve enjoyed the most,” the friend continued. I was planning to go from Pokut to Ayder plateau and then to Yukarı Kavrun. However, these friends changed my mind. I’ve been to Yukarı Kavrun many times – more than once, I’ve been there at least 3-4 times. Çat valley was a place I was particularly curious about; I hadn’t ever been there. I just decided, and I set off towards Verçenik plateau. “And there are crater lakes up on the plateau, so I could go for a walk, and I’d set up a tent for the night,” I started to formulate plans in my mind.

### Çat Valley and Verçenik Valley

After a rough road of rocks and dirt, I reached Verçenik Highland. It was about 15:00. Who starts a hike at that hour? Nobody, of course. But I trusted in my superior abilities, as if I could fly straight to the lakes and back. My plan was to return before dark, a walk of no more than 3 hours. As I was getting ready, other people were finishing their hikes. We met some people, and after asking about the trail, how long it took, whether they came often, where they were from, their names, their mothers’ names, their fathers’ names and whether they had a full family registry certificate, I met Osman. He was from İstanbul too. His family had a house in Demirkapı village above Uzungöl in Trabzon, and a summer place higher up. He said he posted regularly in a Facebook group and they walked together. Naturally I didn’t hold back and gave Yola Çıkmalı a plug :). We said see you around and went our separate ways.

I started my walk at 3, and I managed to reach the car as it got dark. I’ve been experiencing these occasional itchy sensations. I’m trying to find something to occupy myself. It would be nice to be in the dark and surrounded by fog, wouldn’t it? Well, when I get to the valley, I’m thinking of setting up a tent, but there’s nowhere to get food in the valley either. I decided it’s best to go to Toşi restaurant/guest house in Çat Valley, where I can eat and sleep in a comfortable bed – with the dream of being there.

Once people have caught up with civilisation, they immediately dive into the internet. They make 2 posts, hand out 3 to 5 likes, and so on. But, when you're out in nature, put your phone away, relax, and don't think about anything, do you? Well, that's just it; man is never satisfied with anything. I go onto Facebook and immediately find Osman, and add him as a friend. He's an informed, experienced person. While I was thinking about benefiting from his experience, I found out that he's also a member of the PasifikSpor club. His name is Osman. Eee, how is that possible? I start wondering. I had started working out at this gym, which was close to home, specifically for the Lycia hike I was going to in September 2014. The gym owner, Yasin abi, was always telling me that there was someone called Osman, who was always going places and would say, "If we meet, I'll introduce you." We didn't meet for six months. Then I went on the Lycia trail and came back, stopped my exercise, stopped trekking, and gained a bit of weight, yet we never met. It turns out Yasin abi was saying the same thing to Osman abi as well.

Oh, I said to myself. After so much time working together in the same gym, we never actually met, until we ran into each other spontaneously at the foot of a mountain. Some call it coincidence, others luck, and still others consider it chance. And now, I’ve finally found Osman abi.

Osman abi has also returned to his village and invited me. He said that if I come, we could go for a walk together. And, I arrived at Osman abi’s village about 3-4 days later. We went for a walk towards the rocky hill (called “Kale”), which was visible in the background in the photo. Because I didn’t have much time, I decided to turn back halfway. There wasn’t even a path. I really need to continue with the trip, but I don’t have time. Meanwhile, they continued all the way to the top, and they even said they saw a bear. What a missed opportunity! As a result of a spontaneous decision, I was able to actually meet Osman abi, and then we were able to go for a walk together – it was a wonderful and unique experience.

If they say the world is small, then it must be.

After that, I didn’t see Osman abi again :). I don’t even know why.
