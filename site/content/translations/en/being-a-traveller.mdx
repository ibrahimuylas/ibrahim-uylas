Honestly, I don’t know what I am. When I take my backpack and wander, I become a traveler. When I hop on a motorcycle and go on trips, I become a traveler. And when I fly and stay in hotels, I become a tourist. That’s how I would describe myself. Let’s move on to the other questions. I want to be clear from the start that these are somewhat dangerous topics – I don’t intend to criticize anyone, these are just my thoughts. Don’t tell me “no, no, that’s not like that,” please.

### Who is considered a traveler, and how does one become a traveler?

I wasn’t sure how to describe it. There’s no rule that a blond, blue-eyed man over 1.75 metres is a traveller and a woman over 1.65 metres is one too, so it isn’t easy to come up with a neat definition. I’ll try anyway. Travellers are people who have made setting off an addiction or a philosophy of life. They can’t sit still with excitement whenever they make a plan. They gather information about what interests them, keep learning and teach others along the way. The ones who share the details of their trips are especially good travellers. There are people I follow with admiration. You can tell the difference when you meet a traveller. How do you become one? That depends on your circumstances and how much you want it. If someone in your family is ill and you’re responsible for their care, for example, things are harder. If you don’t really want it, you always have an excuse. A traveller finds excuses to set off, not to stay put!

### Does it mean to give up everything and leave in order to become a traveler?

Let’s be honest, my friend, the conditions determine it. It’s easy to just leave everything behind. The real thing is to be a wanderer while fighting for life. Let’s do it, let’s see.

### Is the best traveler also the one who travels the most?

I absolutely disagree. If he’s good at what he does, then he’s even better. In short, it requires hard work.

### Is it good to be a traveler?

You must be thinking about that. It’s incredibly useful, like water. They improve their lives by sharing it, and it doesn’t harm anyone. They live quietly and on their own.

### Is a traveler, a wanderer, and a tourist the same thing?

No. I gave an example myself above. I think that’s enough. I don’t want to prolong this further.
