Nurgaz is one of the first cartridges I ever bought for my camping stove. Because it is filled with 100% butane gas – also known as "strike gas" – I would say its efficiency is lower compared to cartridges filled with 30% propane. Propane-mixed cartridges work more efficiently. Although there may not be a significant difference in the summer months, propane-mixed cartridges are more effective during spring when the weather cools down slightly and when camping at higher altitudes. I can say that a 250gr Nurgaz camping cartridge is sufficient to cover the food and tea needs of two people for a 3–4 day period in spring.

A product suitable for use, especially during the summer months, and one that prevents you from climbing to very high places.
