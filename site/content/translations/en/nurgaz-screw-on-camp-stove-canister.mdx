---
title: "Nurgaz Screw-On Camp Stove Canister"
description: "How a 250 g Nurgaz butane canister worked for two of us cooking and making tea on a spring camping trip."
---
Nurgaz was one of the first canisters I bought for my camping stove. It’s filled with pure butane, the gas you get in a lighter. In my experience it doesn’t perform as well as a blend containing 30% propane. I notice little difference in summer, but the propane mix works better in cool spring weather and higher up. In spring, one 250 g Nurgaz canister lasted the two of us three to four days of cooking and making tea.

This one is mainly for summer use, on trips where you won’t be going too high.
