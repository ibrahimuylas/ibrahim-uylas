It’s a lovely seaside town about 420 km from İstanbul. With its golden sand and calm water that deepens gradually, it feels like a beautiful bay in the Aegean. There are plenty of hotels and guesthouses, and room to pitch a tent if you’d like to spend 2 or 3 days by the sea. June and September tend to be quieter.

### Çakrazşeyhler Beach: Where is it and how do you get there?

It’s some distance from İstanbul, but it’s a good choice if you have about 3 days off and feel like going somewhere different. Drive via Karabük, Safranbolu and Amasra. You’ll arrive about 14 km after passing Amasra. The road is paved, and the stretch from Safranbolu to Çakrazşeyhler is especially enjoyable.

### Çakrazşeyhler Beach Camping Area Information

##### Available facilities

* You can camp at Dolunay Camping, located on the furthest right side of the beach. You can find more details at [Dolunay Camping](http://www.amasra.net/dolunay-camping-cakraz.html).
* Restaurant
* Café
* Merchandise sellers, including those selling gifts.
* Grocery shop
* Toilets
* Parking
* Hotel, guesthouse
* Mosque
