On a London evening, I was slowly cycling through a crowd of people who had jumped onto my bicycle. After a while, a person who seemed intent on stealing my bicycle quickly approached me and, in an instant, pulled out a knife and stabbed it into the middle of my back, running away. I was trying to understand what had happened and found myself lying on the ground. People who had witnessed the event gathered around me in a panic. “Please, call the police and ambulance,” I pleaded. Then, I was trying to assess the depth of the wound, how much blood I was losing, and what my condition was. I couldn’t move, and I couldn’t feel anything. I asked the first person who came up to me, “How does my back feel?” and they told me that I had lost a lot of blood. I lay down on top of my backpack, trying to apply pressure to reduce the bleeding. At that moment, someone said, “It’s no more than 6 minutes.” I knew that within 6 minutes, no help would reach me, because the wound was deep and I was losing a lot of blood. I was trying to figure out what to do. I grabbed the first person who came up to me’s right hand with my right hand, pulling them towards me. I thought, “This is probably my last moments in this world.” I wasn’t sad that I was dying early, but I was thinking that I had lived enough. While being so close to death, I wasn’t panicking because I knew that this day would come. I wanted you to tell them that I thanked everyone who had entered my life and all my loved ones, my family, because our paths crossed, we met, and we created beautiful memories during our short lives. I wanted to say that I was very happy that they had a place in my story, that I loved them very much. More life… then, the surroundings started to darken, the sounds faded, and I said to myself, “The journey has begun.” I slowly released the person’s hand. Then, I opened my eyes, realizing that I was falling asleep while reading my book, as I was heading towards eternity in peace. 

This might have been a dream, but if I only had the last 6 minutes in the world, and I needed to say something, I would say things like this. So, I wanted to write down my dream, even without washing my face :)

May we experience many more beautiful days, knowing the value of life...
