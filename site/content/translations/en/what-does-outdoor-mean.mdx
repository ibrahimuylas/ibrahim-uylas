---
title: What Does “Outdoor” Mean?
description: How the English word “outdoor” made its way into Turkish and came to refer to outdoor sports, clothing, gear and shops.
---

The English word ***outdoor*** made its way into Turkish unchanged. Its literal meaning and how we use it are actually quite different. In Turkish, it translates as “outside” or “in the open air,” but we use it to refer to outdoor sports. Think outdoor clothing, outdoor gear and outdoor shops.
