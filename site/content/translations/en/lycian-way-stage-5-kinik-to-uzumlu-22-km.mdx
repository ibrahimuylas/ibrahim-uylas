## Kınık–Üzümlü: Roads from Çavdır through the villages

This page summarizes the fifth stage, which covers 22 km from Kınık to Çavdır, and then on to Üzümlü. The stage is described as a predominantly forest and road route, and is noted as being easier to walk than previous trails, having begun in June 2014.

> Historical field record: The following practical information is based on the experience of 24 September 2014. Water, facility, transport, and camping conditions should not be considered current information.

### Stage summary

- **Stage:** 5 of 11
- **Start and finish:** Kınık – Üzümlü
- **Waypoints:** Çavdır and Üzümlü villages
- **Distance:** 22 km based on signposted distances
- **Walking time:** The total walking time is unknown. In my journal I noted that I set off from Kınık at about 13:00.
- **Walked:** 24 September 2014
- **Current conditions:** No recent verification was available for this guide.

### Terrain, difficulty and navigation

The 2014 guide describes a short forest track followed by a road. My journal says I missed the trail junction in the forest and stayed on the road to avoid putting more strain on my sore foot. The path I described in the journal should therefore not be taken as the full official route.

### Water and food — 2014 record

At this stage, it is documented that there is no water shortage and that logistical support can be obtained from the villages. In Üzümlü, vineyards and grapes offered by the villagers are part of the daily supply; there is no record of a regular commercial supply point.

### Accommodation, camping and ways off the route — 2014 record

In Kınık, a suitable location for tents could not be found, and a flat area was selected within the forest near Üzümlü for the night. Regular accommodation and options for transfers within the route were not recorded.

### Notable places

Kınık valley, Çavdır, Üzümlü village and its vineyards are distinctive landmarks.

### What I learned on this stage

The journal for the fifth day shows why it’s worth looking for a campsite before dark when you start in the afternoon. The author had a rule about not walking after 18:00 and camped just beyond Üzümlü.
