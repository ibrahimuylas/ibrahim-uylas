### Where is Papaz çayırı, and how do you get there?

You can reach Papaz Çayırı via two different routes. The first is: Kocaeli -> Yuvacık to Aytepe, and then following the signs for Menekşe Highland. The second route is: Kocaeli -> Gölcük -> Nüzhetiye, again following the signs for Menekşe Highland. The first route transitions to a dirt road after Aytepe, and you’ll sometimes need to drive slowly to avoid scraping your car. The second route is smoother than the first. Papaz Çayırı, Menekşe, and Menekşe Subatımı meadows are very close by.

### Papaz çayırı campsite information

##### Available facilities

- Camp Site (You can set up your tent in the flat areas you find. There is no private business.)
- Clean water
- A very natural and pleasant environment. This is, without a doubt, my favorite place.

##### Facilities that are unavailable

- Toilets
- Grocery shop
- Restaurant
- Café

Note: This information dates back to 2013.

### Hiking routes from Papaz Çayırı

#### From Aytepe and Soğukpınar via Papaz Çayırı to Menekşe Highland (11.5 km)

- The route begins in Aytepe, and first you descend to Soğukpınar. From there, you then follow a steep, forest trail to Papaz Çayır and then on to Menekşe Highland. Afterwards, you follow the same path back to your starting point.
- There is water in Soğukpınar, Papaz Çayırı, and Menekşe Highland.
- The forest trail between Soğukpınar and Kayaklıklar might be a bit tiring for you. However, it’s generally a moderate route for beginners and an easy one for experienced hikers.

- Starting on the route from Aytepe, you first descend to Soğukpınar. From there, you then follow a steep, forest trail to Papaz Çayır and then on to Menekşe Highland. Finally, you descend via a forest path to Servetiye Karşı village.
- There is water in Soğukpınar, Papaz Çayırı, and Menekşe Highland.
- The forest trail between Soğukpınar and Kayaklıklar might be a bit tiring, as it’s a medium-level route and quite long.

- From Kırıntı village, follow the dirt road to Menekşe Highland and then Papaz Çayırı. From there, a forest path takes you to Soğukpınar.
- In the villages of Kırıntı, Soğukpınar, Papaz Çayırı, and Menekşe Highland, there is water.
- Overall, it’s a relatively easy course.

- You begin the route at Aytepe, following a steep, forest trail to Menekşe Highland. Then, you retrace the same path, reaching Servetiye Village, descending from Soğukpınar, and subsequently arriving at Karaaslan camping area.
- There are water points on the road.
- A moderate course. Its length can be tiring.

- There are water points along the trail.
- The journey begins in Aytepe and extends to both Soğukpınar and Papaz Çayır. From Soğukpınar, you take a steep, forest trail to reach Papaz Çayır. You may find this a bit tiring. Afterwards, you create a loop on a forest dirt path and return to Aytepe via a different route.
- 25km in length, this is a moderately difficult course. It could be challenging for beginners.

- There are water points along the trail.
- The journey begins in Aytepe and extends to Soğukpınar, and then to Papaz Çayırı. From Soğukpınar, you take a steep, forest trail, which can be tiring. Afterwards, you will proceed through a forest dirt path, passing by the Menekşe Subatımı Highland, and then descend into the forest towards Nüzhetiye.
- A moderately difficult course. It could challenge beginners.

- There are numerous water points along the trail.
- Starting from the Yuvacık junction, you walk along the side of the road to reach Karaaslan camping. Afterwards, you continue along the same path to Servetiye Village, and then to Soğukpınar in Aytepe, via an old forest path. From there, you proceed uphill to Papaz Çayırı and then to Sansarak village. After passing through Sansarak village, you will reach İznik.
- This is a medium-difficulty course. The length of the route may be challenging. You may prefer to complete it in sections, rather than all at once.
