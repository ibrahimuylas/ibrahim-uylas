Also known as Nebiler Waterfall, it is located in Dikili, İzmir. This beautiful waterfall is like a hidden paradise within the forest. Although it is part of İzmir, its distance from İzmir is comparable to that of Balıkesir and Manisa. During my day trip, I went to this place and it exceeded my expectations. You reach the other waterfalls and caves via a lovely forest path. Despite having a somewhat rundown business, the beauty of the waterfall is enough. While the business has tried to maintain things, the services didn't seem very adequate. They charge 5TL upon arrival by car. You can see the different shades of orange and green in autumn.

### Where is Aşıklar Waterfall, and how do you get there?

While traveling on the road from İzmir to Çanakkale, after passing the Dikili fork, you will continue towards Gökçeağıl and Nebiler villages. After entering this road, you will reach the waterfall in approximately 5 km. There are waterfall signs on the road. It’s mostly asphalt, with only a very small section being a dirt road. So, you won’t have any trouble with transportation – it’s a distance that can be covered by hitchhiking or walking – 5 km from the main road is easily walked in about an hour :)

### Aşıklar Şelalesi campsite information

##### Available facilities

- You can leave your car in the designated parking area at the entrance to the lake waterfall area.
- The business charges a 5 TL entrance fee. However, they stated that campers do not need to pay.

  - You can set up camp in a suitable location, or you can continue towards the furthest waterfall, encountering vast, open areas along the way. It would be more comfortable to set up your tent in these areas. However, you’ll need to transport your belongings.

- Due to its ease of access and proximity to the city center, it will attract a large number of weekend day-trip visitors.
- Because it’s a business, there are water and toilets available.
- I haven’t tried the restaurant’s food. There wasn’t even a decent table, really. Perhaps they closed the season because of the autumn weather. But don’t expect too much.
- I recommend that you resolve all of your issues in Dikili or in the Altınova area.
- There are picnic tables at the waterfall where the business is located. It doesn't matter if you find them empty.

##### Facilities that are unavailable

- Grocery shop
- Hotel, Guesthouse

Note: This information dates back to September 2016.
