---
title: "What Is Scree?"
description: "Scree is the loose rock that covers some mountain slopes. Here’s what that means when you encounter it on a hike."
---

You might have heard someone describe a hiking route as having *çarşak*. Fair enough, but what on earth does that mean?

Here’s how Wikipedia defines it.

**Scree** is a slope, usually below a large mass of rock or a rock face in the mountains, covered in fragments that have broken away from the rock above.

Here’s my own definition.

**Scree** is a slope where you can’t see the soil because the ground is covered in rocks of every size. There’s so much loose stone you won’t see a patch of earth a handspan wide. It’s tiring, too. You might want to kiss the ground once you’re across. I know because I did. I’ve basically just described the photo above. That’s what it’s like.
