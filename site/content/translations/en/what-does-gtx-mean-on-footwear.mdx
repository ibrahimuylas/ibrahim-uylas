I’m browsing the internet, looking at trekking shoes. As always, the prices have gone up by a little. Actually, as the dollar and euro rise, the prices of outdoor products are also increasing. Anyway, while looking at the shoes, I noticed that many models have “GTX” written on them. It reminded me so much of GoreTex. I wondered, “Is it GoreTex?” I decided to look at the product descriptions. Unfortunately, here, nobody writes a proper description of the products they sell, except for some outdoor stores. Let's not take that lightly. Then, I decided to consult Google. I searched for “What does GTX shoes mean, what is GTX?” and there were a lot of results from the comments. When I looked for the English translation, as always, I found a lot more resources.

GTX means GoreTex, that’s what the older guys said. They’ve come up with a cooler abbreviation. Just so you know, it might be useful somewhere.
