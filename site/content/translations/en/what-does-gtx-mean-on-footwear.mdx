I’m browsing the internet, looking at trekking shoes. As always, the prices have gone up a bit. Actually, as the dollar and euro rise, the prices of outdoor products also go up. Anyway, while looking at shoes, I noticed that many models have “GTX” written on them. It reminded me so much of Gore-Tex. I wondered, “Is it Gore-Tex?” I decided to check the product descriptions. Here, thank goodness, nobody writes a proper description of the products they sell, except for some outdoor stores. Let’s not take that lightly. Then, I decided to consult Google. I searched for “What does GTX shoes mean, what is GTX?” and got a lot of product results, not comments. When I looked up the English version, as always, I found a lot more resources.

GTX means Gore-Tex, that’s what the older guys said. They’ve come up with a cooler abbreviation. Just so you know, it might be useful somewhere.
