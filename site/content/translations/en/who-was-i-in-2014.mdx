### Who is İbrahim Uylaş?

I was born in Erzurum in 1987 and lived there until university. When I had to decide what to study, I chose computer engineering at Eastern Mediterranean University. I’d been drawn to computers and software since watching my uncle as a child. With financial and emotional support from my family, I graduated in 2010. Thanks again to all of them. I started looking for work as soon as I graduated. In July 2010, I began the working life I’d been looking forward to as a .NET software specialist at [Obase Bilgisayar](http://www.obase.com/). After about 2.5 years of good, enjoyable days in a workplace that felt like a family, I continued my career as a software specialist at [TEB](http://www.teb.com.tr/). My work history is on [LinkedIn](http://tr.linkedin.com/in/ibrahimuylas).

Summarize Me

I’m a computer engineer who’s passionate about trekking, loves nature, is a traveler, an adventurer, and works with software.

When and how did I begin?

I would always look at people who went camping and traveled the roads with their backpacks. This was a demanding sport, but it was beautiful. It was very brave to distance oneself from civilization and be completely alone with nature and oneself. I made a point of not admiring those people who traveled the mountains, exhausted by cars, with their backpacks. It would be nice to start from somewhere, but I had absolutely no idea about this sport. After chatting with a few friends who were camping, I gained some knowledge, and I began to believe that I could do it. Camping/trekking started to feel like a wonderful meal, a scent I could detect from a distance. All I needed was a spark to light the fire, and I found it. With an experienced friend, I started my first camping life in July 2011, with the East Black Sea, after acquiring the first camping equipment. Walking in the forest, setting up a tent wherever I wanted at night, building a campfire, watching the sky and listening to the sounds of wind and water, and, best of all, feeling like I was living in harmony with nature, were all the reasons why I became deeply passionate about trekking. Afterwards, a trekking “monster” emerged within me.

Where will I go?

No matter where – actually, what’s important to me is how long my vacation is, not exactly where I am. Based on how long my vacation is, I find places to go. Because I live in İstanbul, I mostly go to places near İstanbul.

What are my dreams

When I discover a new and wonderful place, or hear about one from someone, it represents a new dream for me. I believe that people should always have dreams. My biggest dream is to create trekking maps by visiting all the cities in the country I love. It might seem a little utopian, but I believe that I will have the strength and courage to realize this dream when the opportunity arises.
