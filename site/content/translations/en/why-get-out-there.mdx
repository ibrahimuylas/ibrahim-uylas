In these first months of 2021, www.yolacikmali.com is slowly becoming part of my past. I decided to carry on as İbrahim Uylaş. I still want to set off and spend my time on the road. In fact, I’ve decided to travel the world and live on the road along the way.

What did I have to say about it on 15 May 2014? Here goes.

Money, time and energy. That wonderful trio never shows up together. As a student, you have time and energy but no money. You finish school and start earning, and suddenly you have money and energy but no time. Then you retire and find you have money and time but no energy. So don’t wait for all three to come together. With a little money, a little energy and a little time, you already have what you need to set off.

### **Why get out there?**

* To travel and see things
* To discover new places
* To go where nobody’s been before (assuming there’s anywhere left)
* To go somewhere you’ve never been
* To keep moving without looking back
* To get back to nature
* To hope to spot rare animals (things change if they spot you first :))
* To go camping
* To go trekking
* To light a campfire
* To fall asleep to the sound of water and wind
* To sing by the fire and watch the stars
* To walk with nowhere in particular to go
* To cross a mountain
* To climb to the summit
* To watch the sunrise or sunset
* To walk through a forest
* To climb a tree
* To step into a waterfall
* To swim in a lake
* To look for adventure and excitement
* To get away from civilisation
* To watch the waves
* To watch the clouds and the sky
* To watch the wind dance with the leaves
* To have fun
* To have a good time
* To clear your head
* To have a conversation with yourself
* To listen to your soul
* To find some peace
* To spend some time alone

You might have other reasons, or you might have none. When it comes to setting off, what you’re looking for is a chance. When you find it, get out there.
