A highland pasture lying at the intersection of the borders of Bolu and Düzce. Although it technically falls within Bolu's borders, it's a yayla where residents of Düzce's Çarkısayvan village live, so I associate this yayla with Düzce. Within the official boundaries, though, it's a very beautiful yayla belonging to Bolu.

### Where is Sinekli Highland, and how do you get there?

When traveling on the TEM highway, you’ll see the Abant sign and then take the exit to go directly to Abant Lake. Approximately 2 km before entering the Abant National Park entrance, you will pass by the Abant fish farms and then follow the dirt road to the right, which is about 3 km long, to reach the valley. You can also arrive here via Düzce – Samandere. However, even during the spring season, there is snow here, and the road coming from Samandere can be closed due to snow. For those coming from İstanbul, this route is certainly closer, but even on May 1st, there was snow here, and vehicles couldn't pass due to the snow.

### Sinekli Highland campsite information

##### Available facilities

- Camping Area (You can set up your tent in the flat areas, or within the forest. There is no private business.)
- There are many houses located up on the highland pasture. The residents of Yaylada begin to come to the area starting in May.
- You could see snow even on May 1st.
- Çeşme

##### Facilities that are unavailable

- Grocery shop
- Restaurant
- Café

Note: This information dates back to May 2015.

- I started from Sinekli Highland and proceeded through the forest, eventually reaching Yenisefaköy Highland. On 1 May, some sections of this dirt road were completely covered in snow, and large trees had fallen across it. After reaching Yenisefaköy Highland, I continued to Doruk Highland. Then, I followed the dirt road again and returned to Sinekli Highland. There is a high probability of heavy snow in this area until May.
- It’s an easy course.
- There are no water sources along the road, but there are taps in the highland pastures. The water is very good.

#### From Samandere village to Sinekli Highland (14.8 km)

- The route begins in Samandere village. It starts on a dirt road and reaches Sinekli Highland, then continues to Abant Lake.
- The route continues upwards from Samandere to Sinekli Highland, eventually descending afterward.
- People arriving by car via Samandere also use this route. There is a possibility that it will be closed due to snow until May.
- A walking route suitable for winter. It’s important to be aware of wild animals.
- It’s an easy course.
- There’s no water along the trail. Fill your bottles in the village before you set off. There’s also a tap on Sinekli Highland.
