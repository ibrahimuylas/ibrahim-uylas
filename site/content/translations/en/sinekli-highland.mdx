A meadow located at the intersection of the borders of Bolu and Düzce. While technically residing within the Bolu borders, it’s a meadow where residents of Düzce’s Çarkısayvan village reside. However, within official boundaries, it’s a very beautiful meadow belonging to Bolu.

### Where is Sinekli Valley, and how do you get there?

When traveling on the TEM highway, you’ll see the Abant sign and then take the exit to go directly to Abant Lake. Approximately 2 km before entering the Abant National Park entrance, you will pass by the Abant fish farms and then follow the dirt road to the right, which is about 3 km long, to reach the valley. You can also arrive here via Düzce – Samandere. However, even during the spring season, there is snow here, and the road coming from Samandere can be closed due to snow. For those coming from İstanbul, this route is certainly closer, but even on May 1st, there was snow here, and vehicles couldn't pass due to the snow.

### Sinekli Yaylası campsite information

##### Available facilities

- Camping Area (You can set up your tent in the flat areas, or within the forest. There is no private business.)
- There are many houses located in Yaylada. The residents of Yaylada begin to come to the area starting in May.
- You could see snow even on May 1st.
- Çeşme

##### Facilities that are unavailable

- Grocery shop
- Restaurant
- Café

Note: This information dates back to May 2015.

- I started from Sinekli yaylası and proceeded through the forest, eventually reaching Yenisefaköy yaylası. On May 1st, a significant portion of the road in this area was completely covered in snow. And there were large trees fallen on the road. After reaching Yenisefaköy yaylası, I continued to Doruk yaylası. Then, I followed the dirt road again and returned to Sinekli yaylası. There is a high probability of heavy snowfall in this area until May.
- It’s an easy course.
- There are no water sources along the road, but there are wells in the meadows. The water there is very good.

#### From Samandere village to Sinekli Valley (14.8 km)

- The route begins in Samandere village. It starts on a dirt road and reaches Sinekli Valley, then continues to Abant Lake.
- The route continues upwards from Samandere to Sinekli Valley, eventually descending afterward.
- People arriving by car via Samandere also use this route. There is a possibility that it will be closed due to snow until May.
- A walking route suitable for winter. It’s important to be aware of wild animals.
- It’s an easy course.
- There are no water sources on the road. You should collect your water in the village before setting out. There is a well located in Sinekli Valley.
