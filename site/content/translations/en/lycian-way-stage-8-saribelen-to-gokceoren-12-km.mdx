## Sarıbelen–Gökçeören: Trail stage in the rain

This guide summarizes the eighth stage, which stretches from Sarıbelen to Gökçeören – a distance of 12 km. Key details from the 2014 hike include a scenic trail, a break offered at the single house along the route, and slippery stones due to rain.

> **Historical field recording:** This information is based on the experience of 27 September 2014. Water, facility, weather, and camping conditions should not be considered current information.

### Stage summary

- **Stage:** 8 of 11
- **Start and finish:** Sarıbelen – Gökçeören
- **Waypoints:** A mountain house along the route and Gökçeören village
- **Distance:** 12 km based on signposted distances
- **Walking time:** The total walking time was not recorded.
- **Walked:** 27 September 2014
- **Current conditions:** No recent verification was available for this guide.

### Terrain, difficulty and navigation

This brief guide note describes the activity as calm, referencing a diary entry written on the same day. The trail was entirely off-road and felt particularly challenging due to accumulated fatigue. The historical record also notes that the stones were slippery after the rain, that the phone signal was unavailable, and an increased risk of falling.

### Water and food — 2014 record

The author has stated that they have not experienced a water shortage. They wrote that they received meals and tea for payment in a house approximately two hours’ walk from Gökçeören, and that they ate at a pension at the end of the project. The current status of these locations has not been verified.

### Accommodation, camping and ways off the route — 2014 record

I pitched my tent in a guesthouse garden in Gökçeören, where I recorded that a shower and food were available. The guesthouse and price information for Sarıbelen was second-hand. No exit options along the stage were identified.

### Notable places

The Sarıbelen exit, the scenic trail on the hillside, and the location of the house on the road, as well as the Gökçeören village endpoint, are all registered points.

### What I learned on this stage

The journal entry for the eighth day describes that the difficulty of a short distance hike wasn't apparent on its own. Accumulated fatigue, rain, and wet stones had significantly altered the conditions of the 12 km stretch.
